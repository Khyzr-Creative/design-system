Design system · Interface

The pattern elements

The two patterns as code and as files. <khyzr-field> draws the mesh and <khyzr-logo-pattern> draws the logo pattern, and every still of either is drawn by the same engine. When to use a background, and where, is the brand's Backgrounds chapter.

The mesh element

One element, two looks: <khyzr-field>, from khyzr-field.js in the design system's pattern assets. It is sized by its container. A WebGL2 shader draws it, and each element holds a canvas of its own.

White type

theme="dark"

<khyzr-field theme="dark">

Reduced motion is on, so the pattern holds one frame.

<script src="design-system/assets/pattern/khyzr-field.js"></script>
<div style="position:relative;overflow:hidden">
  <khyzr-field theme="dark" style="position:absolute;inset:0"></khyzr-field>
  
</div>

Place the element absolutely, filling a positioned container that clips its overflow, and set the content above it.

  • Dark, for white type. The default. Its greens run from pitch through green-900, forest, khyzr-green, and green-400 to green-300.
  • Light, for ink type. Its greens are green-050, green-200, mint, and leaf.

Attributes

AttributeWhat it does
themedark, the default, is the look for white type. light is the look for ink type

  • Only the two looks are the pattern. recipe reaches the rest of the engine, and nothing there is adopted.
  • A script can draw without the element, through KhyzrField: create for a live field on a canvas, still for one frame, and presets for the looks.

The recipe

Both looks are one recipe with two sets of greens. The same recipe draws the same picture at any size, live in a page or as a still.

  • Every color in both looks is a token. The engine carries their values, so a token that changes is changed in the engine too.

Motion and the pointer

  • Drift. The colors move on their own clock, at the recipe's speed. A look always opens on its moment, so the first frame is the same on every load.
  • The pointer glow. Under the pointer the field brightens a little: a glow at strength 0.12, over a radius of 0.21 of the field's shorter side. It eases in as the pointer arrives and out as it leaves. A still field keeps the glow.
  • Still. static holds the look's moment: one frame, no drift.

  • Give the container the look's first color as its ground, or the look's still as its background image. Type on the field then reads before the field arrives, and where it never does.

The logo pattern element

One element, two looks: <khyzr-logo-pattern>, from khyzr-logo-pattern.js in the design system's pattern assets. It is sized by its container. It holds one canvas, redrawn when its box changes size, and it sets its own background to the look's ground.

White type

theme="dark"

Ink type

theme="light"

<script src="design-system/assets/pattern/khyzr-logo-pattern.js"></script>
<div style="position:relative;overflow:hidden">
  <khyzr-logo-pattern theme="dark" style="position:absolute;inset:0"></khyzr-logo-pattern>
  
</div>

Place it as the mesh is placed: absolutely, filling a positioned container that clips its overflow, with the content above it.

  • Dark, for white type. The default. A green-900 ground, with the rings in green-800.
  • Light, for ink type. A green-100 ground, with the rings in green-050 at 30% opacity.
  • Measured on a 16:9 frame, at the worst pixel: white type on the dark look holds 13.2:1, and ink type on the light look 16:1.
AttributeWhat it does
themedark, the default, is the look for white type. light is the look for ink type
presetA look by name. The two looks are the only ones the engine carries
recipeA look of its own, as JSON
  • Only the two looks are the logo pattern. recipe reaches the rest of the engine, and nothing there is adopted.
  • A script can draw the same picture without the element, through KhyzrLogoPattern: draw onto a canvas, still for a new canvas, svg for the picture as SVG paths, layout for the placed pieces, and presets for the looks.

  • A setting a look leaves out takes the engine's default: the square lattice is one, and a fine grain at 0.05 is another.

Its floors

  • Nothing moves. There is no drift and no pointer glow.
  • No JavaScript. The element draws nothing, and its container's own ground shows. Give the container the look's ground, or the look's still.

The stills

Every background is also a still image, for whatever cannot run an element: a slide, a document, a cover, a post, a meeting background. The engines draw them, so a still is the live element held at its moment in a box of the same size.

Names

A still is named khyzr-{background}_{look}_{job}_{width}x{height}, as a PNG, a JPG, or an SVG.

PartWhat it says
Backgroundmesh, logo-pattern, or gradient
Lookdark carries white type and light carries ink type. The gradient is dark-to-light or light-to-dark
JobThe shape, named for its use
SizeIn pixels
  • The PNG is the master. The JPG is its twin for decks, documents, and posts, at a fifth of the weight or less. This site serves the JPG.
  • The logo pattern also comes as SVG, named without a size. The SVG has no grain. Its light look uses an opacity and a mask, which office software does not always honor: there, use the JPG.

  • The mesh and the logo pattern come in every job, in both looks. The gradient comes as a slide and as A4, in both directions.
  • Each shape is its own picture. The engines draw for the box they are given, so a band is not a slice of a slide.
  • On a shape wider than about three to one the mesh opens into separate glows, with the look's first color between them. The calm middle is where type sits.
  • The logo pattern thins toward the lower left, so type has a calm place there.

In a page

  • Set a still as a background image that covers its box, or as an image cropped to it. Scale by the corner and crop. Never stretch, tile, rotate, or recolor one.
  • Type on a dark look is on-dark-heading. Type on a light look is ink. On any background, type is large and brief.
  • The A4 files are the trimmed page, with no bleed. For a bleed, scale the picture up 3% from its center.
  • Every file is sRGB, with the profile embedded.

The share card and the avatars

  • The share card. 1200 × 630: the mesh's dark look with the on-dark horizontal lockup centered, 380px wide. It is the window of the 1920 × 1080 still where the field is nearest pitch under the mark. It carries no tagline.
  • The avatars. The badge with its plate run to the edge of the square, so the platform does the rounding. The symbol is the badge file's own, untouched.

The earlier cutouts

Before the mesh, the pattern was paper cutouts in the brand greens, drawn by a third element. It is retired: nothing draws it, and its file is no longer in the system. A document made now takes a still of the mesh in its cover column (see Documents for builders).
