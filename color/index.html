Brand · The look

Color

Ink and white lead. One green carries the brand, and it is rare. Four secondaries each have one job.

White and ink lead.

A khyzr surface is mostly white, with ink for the type. Green arrives last and small, so that it is always the point.

  • A working ratio, not law: white 45 · ink 40 · green 10 · everything else 5.
  • On a white page, green is the last words of a title, a dot, a rule. About one part in fifty.
  • A piece may end dark: Ink or Pitch for the last page or slide, with white type and one light green accent.

One green.

khyzr Green is the brand's color, and the name's meaning. Its family runs deeper and lighter around it, and each member has a place.

  • khyzr Green is the accent: the green phrase in a title, a rule, a dot, the first series of a chart.
  • Forest and Deep Forest are the dark fields and deep fills.
  • The deep green is darker still: the plate behind the badge, a dark panel, the dark end of the gradient.
  • Mint and Pale Mint are the light: a pale panel, a dot, type on a dark field.
  • Light green is the accent on a dark ground, where khyzr Green would sink: the green phrase in a white title.
  • One green family carries the brand. Photographs are never tinted green.

The neutrals.

Paper is white. Ink is the black that sets every title. Between them sit the grays for reading text, captions, and hairlines.

  • Titles are Ink. Reading text is Body, a softer gray-black. Supporting text is Muted, a mid gray.
  • Light gray sets a section apart from the white around it. Hairline draws the rules and the borders.
  • Pitch is the darkest ground. It is never a text color.

Four secondaries, each with a job.

Cobalt informs, marigold warms, terracotta signals, steel quiets. A secondary is a moment: an accent, a wash, a mark on a chart.

  • One at a time, and green still reads first.
  • Never side by side as competing fields. Never the color that carries the brand.
  • In a chart, green is the first series and terracotta only flags. See Data.

The gradient.

Dark to light, JPGLight to dark, JPG

One approved ramp, from Pitch through the greens to white. Use it as it is, and never take a new color from it.

  • It runs top to bottom: Pitch, the deep green, Forest, Bright leaf, Mint, Pale Mint, white. It may also run mirrored.
  • It has two jobs: a full-bleed cover, and the turn between a dark page and a light one. See Backgrounds.
  • Bright leaf lives only inside the gradient and the light mesh. It is not a color to pick.

Color in print.

Screens show khyzr's deep greens brighter than process ink can print them. Plan for it.

  • khyzr Green, Forest, Deep Forest, and the deep green print a little duller and yellower than they look on screen.
  • Where the green must be exact, a cover or a sign, ask the printer for a spot match against a printed proof.
  • The secondaries, the grays, and the light greens print as they look.
  • Every value for screen and for print is in Color values.
