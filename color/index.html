02 · Foundations

Color

The Verdant standard — the deep green in the lead, with cobalt, marigold, terracotta and steel as working secondaries. Around it sit the functional website palette, a brand palette for design work, and the approved hero gradient.

Brand core

From the logo: the four greens of the Verdant standard, adopted 2026-08-08, plus the black and the paper they sit on. One green family carries the brand; photos are never green-tinted.

  • khyzr-green is the primary brand green. forest is the deep green for hover, pressed states and deep fills. deep-forest is the deepest solid. mint is the light sage.
  • ink is the workhorse black, for headings. paper, the page canvas, is white since 2026-09-29.
  • The logo art keeps its own brighter cut of the same hue — #149314 · #136E13 · #0C4A0C · #5CBA5C, the original marks' value and saturation rotated to the adopted green. Every UI color derives from the tokens.
  • Take mark colors from the mark files and palette colors from the tokens. Never recolor either to match the other.

Secondaries

True secondaries, not just functional. Cobalt informs, marigold warms, terracotta signals, steel quiets.

  • Use them creatively across pages and designs — accents, washes, graphic moments — as long as green reads first.
  • Never set them side by side as competing fields, and never let one carry the brand.
  • Data-viz discipline is unchanged: green is series 1, and terracotta only flags.
  • Grades are measured on Paper. Where a color can't carry copy, its darkened text twin is named, and the twin governs it as copy.

Neutrals

The canvas, surfaces and text greys.

  • paper is the page background; surface carries cards; surface-2 carries the alternate sections; hairline draws the borders.
  • faint is for captions, muted for secondary text, body for body text, ink for headings.
  • The text greys keep a green cast. The canvas, the alt surface and the hairline are neutral since 2026-09-29, so cards on the canvas separate by their hairline.
  • pitch is a surface color — dark sections and the gradient anchor. It is never a text color.

Hero gradient

The hero ramp is a brand-approved artifact. Use it as shipped; don't derive UI colors from it.

  • Bright leaf exists only in this ramp. Pitch doubles as the dark-surface neutral.
  • It runs top to bottom: pitch, deep green, forest, bright leaf, mint, pale mint, white.
  • gradient-hero interpolates in OKLab; gradient-hero-srgb is the same ramp interpolated in sRGB.
  • On the hero itself the ramp is drawn in a shader. The token is its flat equivalent, for use anywhere else.

Accessibility

Floors in force

  • Reading copy is body or muted, never faint. Faint is for captions and labels.
  • green-700 is available for emphatic text.
  • Marigold never sets text; its twin marigold-text does.
  • Terracotta text always uses the twin terracotta-text. Terracotta itself is for chart marks, flags and large text.
  • Cobalt is the only text-safe secondary.
  • The steel tint (viz-4) is a chart shape beside labelled values, never text.
  • On green-900: white for headings, on-dark-body and on-dark-soft for reading, on-dark-muted for labels and legal, green-bright for accent words. Terracotta is marks only on dark; steel and the steel tint serve as graphics.
  • muted and cobalt never sit on green-900 — they sink.
  • Badges set Deep Forest on Pale Mint. The primary also passes on Pale Mint, so badge text may go primary or Deep Forest.
  • A signal chip sets the text twin on the terracotta wash (signal-tint), bold or large only.
