Design system · As built

khyzr.studio as built

The khyzr.studio site as it stands. Where its color lives, its type, space, and corners, its depth and its motion, the hero, every section in page order, and the site-wide pieces. The product's mark and color are its brand chapter, and the frame it shares with khyzr.ai is written once.

Shared and its own

  • With khyzr.ai: the frame, the seams, the type scale, the nav, the closing, the colophon, focus, the button skeletons, the radius ladder, the scroll map, the eased wheel, and the scroll entrance, shared by name and structure. See The product frame.
  • Its own: color, the hero, the engines, every section's content, and the timings of its motion.
  • Held in the system: every value on this page is in tokens/products/khyzr-studio.css, a checked copy that states the commit it was read at.

Where color lives

The ramp, its five steps, and what each means are on the product's brand chapter, khyzr.studio.

  • How much: the page is about two thirds black. Warm near-black takes 12%, gray and bone 5%, and ember 12%, of which the membership band is about 10. Outside that band, ember is about two parts in a hundred. The remaining 5% is the pictures' own colors.
  • ink-1 is the first step up from black. The services band and the closing plate stand on it, and the tones mixed over the ground mix from it. ink-2 is the second, under the benefits' art before its picture arrives.
  • The brand color is accent, which reads ember-1. Every flat accent reads it: the wordmark's .studio, the focus ring, selection, link and nav underlines, hover borders, the plan's ticks and its tag.
  • hot reads ember-3: the membership band's base, and the upper rule of an open question.
  • The five pillars take the ramp's five steps in order, ember-1 to ember-5 (tint): each row's badges, its icon, its glow, and its progress rule in the services; the pillar's line in the work's hover label; the spine of the pass.
  • One page-scale color commit: the membership band.
  • Texture: the dot grid lives in the closing band only; grain is the one full-viewport texture.
  • Contrast: text holds 4.5:1 or more; ember is a state, not text. On black the five steps hold 13.7, 10.6, 8.1, 6.0, and 4.4:1. Hairlines sit at 1.30:1 on the black ground. The nav's links are set halfway from ash to bone (nav-link), so they hold over whatever passes under the frost.

The gradients

  • The ember field: a base of ember-3 under a conic turn between mixes of ember-2 and ember-1, with a pool of ember-4 at the bottom left and at the leaving edge. It turns once in 26s. At most moments it reads amber, nearer ember-2 than the ember-3 it is built on.
  • The primary button's hover: ember-1, then ember-2 at 40%, ember-3 at 68%, and ember-2 again, drifting.
  • The rim: a conic light of ember-4, ember-3, ember-1, and ember-2, over a little more than two fifths of the lap.
  • The closing's light: a core of ember-1, with ember-2 at 30% and ember-3 at 80%.
  • The scroll map's marker: ember-1 into ember-2.
  • The hero's object: black through the hairline tone into ember-2 and ember-1.

Colors off the ramp

Each belongs to one object or one picture. None is a token, and none travels.

  • The journey fields: three generated pictures (golden yellow, electric blue, vermilion), each with a soft bloom across the spectrum and its own grain.
  • On those fields: copy in #17100a on the yellow; white under a shadow of rgba(46,8,3,.6) on the vermilion; the request pills in ivory, rgba(255,250,238,.84), with #17100a words.
  • The benefits' icons: white into a pale frosted blue-white, rgba(214,244,255,.9).
  • The warm shadow on the ember field: rgba(58,16,4) at 46 to 70%, under the pass and the plan.
  • The pass's hardware: a strap of #141210, a clip in four dark metals, and a holographic seal in six pastels.
  • The badge tones in the services: bone mixed into ink-1 in nine steps, from 4.5 to 52%.
  • The deep tile, #3B2300, in the favicon and the app icons only.
  • Clients' marks are repainted in one ink, bone with a seventh of ember-2. The work's pictures keep their own colors.

The radius ladder

  • One family, three weights, and these sizes only on the page itself. Line height and tracking for each role are on The product frame.
  • Off the scale, by design: the pillars' names in the services, 67px at full width and 27px on a phone, at 600 on a line height of 1 with −.045em; the pass's print, set in the pass's own units; and the Arabic name in the colophon, in Noto Naskh Arabic as Type sets it.
  • Display, tightened: the membership band's headline runs at .98 with −.04em.
  • Measures: the hero's headline 16ch, a featured head 18ch, a continuation 34ch, a lede 48ch, body 62ch, an answer 66ch, a benefit's description 28ch, the footer's line 32ch, legal prose 64ch.
  • Emphasis is by ink, not weight: bone inside ash, at one weight.
  • Corners in use: the hero's action 4px; every standard button 6px; collage tiles, the wall's sheets, and the join button 8px; service badges 12px; journey panels, benefit art, the plan, and the services' lit band 16px; the request pills alone are pills. r-2xs is on the ladder and read by nothing.
  • Lines (hairlines, ruled rows) and the bands that span the screen stay square.
  • The membership pass is off the ladder by design: its corners are ratios of the pass, 6% of its width for the holder and 2.2% for the print.

Depth and light

  • Hairlines carry the structure: 1px in hair under the nav, between questions, around secondary buttons, down the quotes' gutter, above the footer's last line, and as the closing's 64px rule. Inside the plan they are bone at 9%; on the ember band, black at 20%.
  • An edge on a picture: a 1px line inside it, bone at 5 to 10%, keeps a dark tile reading as a tile. Journey panels carry a border of bone at 24% and a highlight inside their top edge.
  • Shadow under type on a picture: offset black in two layers, 0 2px 3px at 35% and 0 6px 18px at 45%. Never a glow.
  • Shadow under an object on black: 0 24px 54px -38px at black 88% under a journey panel; 0 8px 14px -8px at black 50% under the wall's sheets; 0 12px 28px -14px at black 70% under a hover label.
  • Shadow under an object on ember is warm, not gray: 0 30px 60px -34px rgba(58,16,4,.7) under the plan.
  • Frost: the nav, as The product frame sets it. The plan is smoked ink, the ground at 95% over the ember field. A hover label is the ground at 88% over a 12px blur.
  • Grain: one fixed layer over the whole page at 5% in overlay: fractal noise at a base frequency of .85, three octaves, on a 160px tile.

Motion

  • ease carries state changes, 300ms or less; ease-long carries arrivals. Neither overshoots.
  • The entrance: type rises by the line over 850ms, 60ms apart, and what stands under it follows 12px behind; blocks rise 12 to 32px over 700 to 900ms on ease-long's curve; a sequence arrives 140ms apart, parallel things 45 to 60ms apart. A block shows at once when focus enters it.
  • Response: a press scales to .97. A text link grows a 1px ember underline from the left in 220ms. A click ripples in ember-1 over 550ms. The rim laps an edge once in 1.4s.
  • Between pages: a 220ms cross-fade, where the browser can.
  • Reduced motion: fewer and gentler, not none. Movement goes; the color and opacity feedback that confirms an action stays, at 200ms or less. The hero rests on its still.

Buttons

  • Primary: a bone fill with black words on r-sm. On hover and on focus the fill gives way to the drifting ember gradient; the words stay black.
  • Secondary: no fill, bone words, a hairline border that warms toward the brand color under the pointer.
  • The bar's action: the small size, as a ghost: no fill, bone words, and a quiet border, a fifth of bone over the hairline tone, that warms toward the brand color under the pointer. So the hero's action is the first screen's one lit control.
  • The join button: the membership's one big action. The primary at the plan's full width and half as tall again, on r-md, its label in fs-h3, its arrow in a chip on r-sm that slides under the pointer.
  • The hero's action is its own, described with the hero.

The hero

A live object behind one centered lockup: the object is the argument, the copy its caption.

  • The plate: the screen's width and 82vh tall, with 64px above and below, on the black ground. At 720px and under it is 82svh and at most 700px, with 22px above and 26px below; svh, so it does not grow as the browser's bar folds away. It draws no rule: the nav's rule is its top edge, and nothing divides it from the first section. Its canvas runs on into that section's seam, so the motes drift past the plate's foot.
  • The object, in lacquer: a closed path of twelve anchors, drawn with its construction (key nodes, handles, and the nib that edits it), then filled and raised into a dome of lacquer on a plane in a perspective stage, with motes around it. Its body is black rising through the hairline tone into the ramp's two yellows, its edge melted into the black; the construction is drawn in ash.
  • How it behaves: every two seconds the nib edits it into the next form (circle, pebble, leaf, squircle), whatever the pointer does. A fine pointer can drag its anchors, which holds the cycle while it drags and for 2.4s after; a click reshapes it. The motes hold and float slowly. The loop sleeps while the plate is off screen.
  • The lockup: one centered column: the headline in fs-h1 at a 16ch measure, a two-line lede at 48ch in full bone, and one action, 40px apart. The type carries offset shadows in the ground's own black, never a glow, so it reads over the object.
  • The action: the hero's own button on the shared skeleton, 40px tall on r-xs: a dark button whose 1px border carries a turning light, with a speckle wedge and a sheen in an amber between ember-1 and ember-2, a nib glyph before its label, and a glow under the label on hover and focus only. The light turns once in 3s, slows under the pointer or focus, and stands still while the button is off screen.
  • The arrival: the page's one authored sequence: the object first, the words half a second after, rising by the line, then the action. The copy is at rest by 1.6s and the object still by 2.25s. It is armed before first paint and released by a 1.2s watchdog if nothing takes over.
  • The hand-off: scrolled, the object lifts faster than the page, by 0.3 of the plate; the lockup lifts by the same amount on the same clock and fades with it, so words and object leave as one picture. It stays put where the browser cannot do this, and under reduced motion.
  • Still and reduced motion: one composed still, the pebble with its construction. Without WebGL2 the canvas stays empty and the plate stands with its copy.
  • Narrow plates: on a phone, or a window taller than wide, the object stands above the lockup and the lockup goes to the plate's foot; the object is fitted to the room above the headline, down to 40% of its size. A phone on its side keeps the wide composition.

The page

Twelve blocks in order: the nav, the hero, the in-house statement, the services, the steps, the benefits, the quotes, the work, the membership, the FAQ, the closing, the colophon. A major seam opens each new movement; a minor one binds what is one thought.

  • The section head: the services, the steps, the benefits, the work, and the FAQ open on one shape: a centered statement in fs-h2 over a gray continuation in fs-cont, one column, no rule, head-gap above the content.
  • Three bands span the screen: the hero, the membership, and the closing. Everything else stands on the column, and the work's strip runs to the screen's edges.
  • The action: "Get started" in the bar, in the hero, and in the closing. The join button carries the membership's own words.

The in-house statement

The one section without a head, first under the hero, behind a major seam: a sentence and, under it, a strip of marks. Nothing here is a card, a panel, or a rule.

  • The sentence: one centered line in fs-feat at the UI weight, in ash, with four phrases lifted into bone: tone, not weight.
  • The strip: one run of clients' marks, printed twice and lapped, drifting slowly and dissolving at both ends. Each mark is painted in one ink, bone with a seventh of ember-2, through the file's own shape, so the row reads at one weight.
  • States: the strip holds while the pointer is on it, and the mark under the pointer comes up. Nothing in it takes focus, and it has no pause control. The lap holds while it is off screen.
  • Reduced motion: a composed block, not a frozen strip: the second run goes and the marks wrap centered, so only whole marks show.
  • Phone: under 960px the marks step down a fifth.

The services

A roster of the five pillars and their services, on hairlines.

  • A row: the pillar's name in big type at the left, the page's one graphic exception to the type scale, and its services at the right as skeleton badges, each a small drawing wearing its name under it, in up to two lines. A badge is never under 84px a side.
  • The lit row: a band travels between rows to the active one. It stands on ink-1 and reaches a step past the column where the screen has room. The row's badges take the pillar's ember in turn and rise a pixel or two.
  • States: it cycles every six seconds. Hover and focus hold the cycle; a row selects itself under a moving pointer only; an explicit pick stops the cycle for that page view. Resting pillar names sit at 3.45:1 on black, over the large-text floor.
  • Reduced motion: no cycle.
  • 880px and under: the name takes the row and the badges run under it as a sideways strip to the screen's edges, sized so the fourth shows about two fifths at the edge.

The steps

The method as three journey panels in a row (subscribe, request, receive), arriving as a sequence, 140ms apart.

  • A panel: a media surface on r-xl inside a 1px border, in two rows: an art field, then its copy, the heading in fs-feat. The three headings sit on one line whatever their bodies wrap to.
  • The art: three saturated portrait fields (golden yellow, electric blue, vermilion) whose own grain is their only texture. Copy sits directly on them; the last panel's is white, carried by an offset shadow in the hero's grammar.
  • Subscribe carries the membership pass at a toy's size, hanging from the panel's top edge on its lanyard, 3.5° off plumb. It swings in on arrival and through plumb under a fine pointer.
  • Request is three straight rows of request pills at reading size, each row traveling at its own speed and dissolving at the panel's walls.
  • Receive is a wall of the studio's own work on a plane turned 24°: three lanes of six sheets, the near lane larger and faster. The sheet under the pointer lifts and straightens, and the stream slows to about a third while the pointer is in the panel.
  • At rest: the loops pause off screen. Under reduced motion the pills are composed (whole pills, wrapped centered) and the wall stands still. The three objects are decorative and hidden from assistive technology.
  • Narrower: from 641 to 960px the three panels hold their row and the pill steps down to fs-sm, so a whole one still fits its track. At 640px and under, one wide panel to a row, and the pill returns to reading size.

The benefits

Five cards under the head: a square art field, then its words on the dark page.

  • A card: the art on r-xl, a saturated grainy field with one pale, frosted icon on it; under it the title in fs-h3 and a description in the body role. These fields and their icon material are this section's own, a scoped exception to the one-texture rule.
  • The icons: five filled icons from the Phosphor pack, painted through their own shapes and evened by ink, not by box.
  • Motion: each glyph lifts a few percent of its box and settles, its light breathing with it and a sheen crossing once a cycle, each card a fifth of a lap behind the last. It pauses off screen and is static under reduced motion and without script.
  • Layout: five in a row down to 881px; three over two, the two centered, from 560 to 880px; two up under 560px, the fifth centered. Art, title, and description share rows, so the descriptions in a row start level.

The quotes

A spread of two quotes with no head, running straight on into the work: no seam under it.

  • The spread: two halves divided by a gutter hairline that fades out at both ends. Each half is centered: the sentence in fs-feat at the text weight, then a signature band 48px tall: a face or a drawn mark, with a name and a role. Nothing is boxed, and no quotation glyph is drawn.
  • 880px and under: the spread stacks, and the gutter turns with it.

The work

A head, then one collage of the studio's work.

  • The collage: it runs to the screen's edges, cut clean at both ends, its first picture starting on the column. Pictures keep their own proportions, 8px apart, at a height of three tenths of the screen's width, between 300 and 680px. A hairline inside each picture's edge keeps a dark one reading as a tile.
  • How it moves: it drifts, about 55px a second at 1440px, and opens partway through its loop. A visit rearranges the same selection.
  • Only a hand holds it: a press stops it; a drag, a sideways wheel, or an arrow key moves it; left alone for 5s it eases back to its pace over 2.4s. Keyboard focus holds it. Hovering never does.
  • Hover: a label in the picture's lower corner (the pillar with its icon, the service, the brand) and a lift in exposure. Some pictures play a short logo sting.
  • Entrance: its tiles are laid out left to right, 32ms apart.
  • Reduced motion, or no script: a sideways scroller, and animated pictures show their stills.

The membership

The page's one color commit: a band the screen's width on the ember field, fitted to one screen under the nav from 1024 × 768 to 1920 × 1080.

  • The field: it turns slowly, one turn in 26s, in small steps, only while its section is the current one, and stays between ember-3 and ember-1, with a deep pool of ember-4 at the bottom left and at the leaving edge. Black holds 7.5:1 on ember-3 and 5.6:1 on the pool.
  • Three columns, as tall as the pass: the pitch, the pass, the plan. Two objects stand on the field, the pass and the plan.
  • The pitch: the headline in fs-h1, three lines, its capitals on the plan's top edge; its lede; and two assurances ruled in black, each mark in a small black tile with a yellow mark, ending on the plan's bottom edge. The product's name inside the headline is typed, not drawn.
  • The plan: a sheet of smoked ink, the page's ground at 95%, so a little of the field shows through. Bone and ash inside, as on the page; yellow only for its marks and its tag. What is included runs as ruled rows that take the sheet's spare height, their ticks drawn down the list on arrival. At its foot, an invitation in fs-h1 with its terms, then the join button.
  • The anchor: a jump to the membership lands the band flush under the nav, not the seam above it.
  • 960px and under: one column: the pass, the pitch as its caption, the plan, the assurances.

The membership pass

The membership's object: one drawing, mounted in the band and, small, in the steps.

  • The object: a printed pass in a clear acrylic holder on a black woven lanyard, stitched in the .studio yellow along both edges, the wordmark woven in. Its back is the same pass in black.
  • The front: bone paper. The wordmark in one ink, black, both parts. A spine of the five pillars in the ramp's five steps, each with its icon in black. A holographic seal. A black member strip at its foot, its number in the brand color.
  • The back: black, with the wordmark in bone and the brand color.
  • Depth is real: a back plate, four rings of edge, the print, and a front plate, stacked, so a fine pointer's tilt slides the acrylic over the print. The acrylic is told by its edges and the light it catches, never by a fill. Its shadow on the field is warm, not gray.
  • Where it hangs: from its mount's top edge, which cuts the straps. In the band it comes down from the top hairline; it pivots where it hangs.
  • Its moments: on arrival it swings and settles, a band of light crosses the acrylic, and the seal flares; the same light returns rarely, never off screen. The rim laps the holder's edge on arrival and on hover.
  • The turn: a click, a tap, or the one control under it turns it over, on a spring with a 2% overshoot; a second click mid-turn retargets. One face shows at a time; both stay readable to assistive technology.
  • Small: in the steps the same pass hangs at a toy's size, its wordmark in one ink.
  • Reduced motion: it still turns, because the state change is the information, but does not travel, swing, or light.

The FAQ

A head, then an index of questions on hairlines: native disclosure rows. An index, not a sequence, so no numbers; no chevron, no circle, no card.

  • Type: the question in bone at the UI weight, the answer in ash at a 66ch measure, both in fs-body.
  • The mark is two rules. Closed, they make a plus. Open, it lies down and the pair separates to the answer's extent, the upper rule in hot. Under the pointer and on focus the rules turn bone.
  • Opening: the answer's extent animates where the browser can; elsewhere the row simply opens. Under reduced motion it is a 200ms change of opacity.

The closing

A plate the screen's width on ink-1, drawing only its top and foot, one minor seam above the footer.

  • The band: across the plate's top, 140px tall: the dot grid, on a 12px pitch, its dots bone at 15%, lit where a slow ember light passes under it, with a hairline beneath. The light only ever shows as lit dots, never as a glow on the plate.
  • The seal: the symbol bare, in the mark's own colors, on that hairline: 40px, or 32px at 720px and under. The hairline parts for it and the dots clear around it. It blooms once on arrival, pair by pair in the mark's own depth order, and the seam opens with it.
  • The column: one, centered: the line in fs-feat, the lede, a 64px rule, one primary action. sp-2 from line to lede; the rule stands centered with sp-3 either side.
  • The rim: one point of ember light traveling the plate's edge while it is on screen. Off under reduced motion.

The colophon

  • Columns: the brand column, the wordmark and a line, carries the weight; beside it stand three link columns: navigate, legal, and contact, which is the email alone. Their labels are real headings at fs-sm, in bone. At 960px and under the brand takes a row of its own over the three; at 720px and under, one column.
  • The last line: under a rule, the copyright with khyzr's Arabic name and the build date, in the micro role. No rule tops the footer: the seam above it divides it from the page.
  • Touch: the links' tap areas fill their 31px pitch.

Icons

One pack: Phosphor, in its fill cut, as local files.

  • The five pillars, in the ramp's order: pen-nib, browser, megaphone-simple, presentation, film-slate.
  • The five benefits: lock-key, lightning, star, arrows-out, fingerprint-simple.
  • Painted through their own shape, never set as colored files: a pillar's icon takes its step of the ramp, at 35% resting and full when lit; on the pass's spine it is black on ember; on a benefit's field it takes the frosted paint; in a hover label it is 13px in the pillar's step.
  • Drawn in the page: the nib on the hero's action, the two assurance marks in 40px black tiles, the plan's ticks, the arrow in the join button's chip, the turn glyph under the pass.
  • The service badges are small skeleton drawings, 84px, made of tone steps, not pictures.
  • The FAQ's mark is two 1px rules, not an icon. No arrow is typed as a character, and there is no second icon style.

Across the site

  • The scroll map: its clusters take nine colors from each block's own picture, or its inks. The marker is a frosted band with one ember rule. Hover lights a cluster in ember, a click lands its block under the nav, and a drag on the column scrolls the page. A hairline runs down its left edge; above the first block and below the last, its empty ends are clusters in the hairline tone. Under reduced motion it follows the scroll exactly.
  • Grain and the dot grid: grain is one fixed layer over the whole page. The dot grid lives in the closing band alone.
  • Selection and the caret: selection is ember-1 under black text; the caret and the form accent read the brand color. Where the browser's scrollbar shows, it is the hairline tone on black.
  • The marks: the wordmark in the bar and the colophon is bone with .studio in the brand color. The one light surface it meets, the pass's paper, sets it in one ink, #000; the pack's one-color black is #1A1A1A. The light cut, with .studio in ember-3, is used nowhere on the site.
  • khyzr on the page: the product's name, and the Arabic name once, in the copyright line. No khyzr logo, no line of endorsement, and no link to khyzr.com or to khyzr.ai.
  • The lost page: one screen with no nav and no footer: the symbol alone, between 56 and 72px, blooming once on load, over the closing's own column: its type roles, its measures, one action home.
  • The legal pages: three, on the site's frame with the colophon and no nav. None of the site's own behaviors runs there: the framework's scripts load, and nothing else. One centered measure: a head, the title over two lines in the micro role, closed by a hairline, then a heading in fs-h3 and its paragraphs for each section; the imprint's fields in two columns, one on a phone. The prose is ash mixed into bone, quieter than the headings. Links turn ember-2 under the pointer.
  • The social card: 1200 × 630, the site's own first screen as its reduced-motion still: the lacquer form with the headline and lede over it, the nav reduced to its wordmark and the action hidden. The wordmark stands 56px in from the top left at 1.4 times its nav size. The icon is never set beside it. It is remade whenever the hero changes.
  • Icons in use: the favicon as an SVG with a 32px PNG, the home-screen icon, and a web manifest at 192 and 512px with a maskable cut, each the deep-tile badge. On the page the symbol stands bare in two places, the closing's seal and the lost page, and never beside the wordmark. The theme color is #000000.
  • The consent notice is built and switched off.
  • Without script: the page renders complete and nothing is hidden. The collage is a sideways scroller in its saved order, the phone's nav stays two rows, the pass shows its front, the seal stands whole and open, and the browser's own scrollbar shows.
  • Phone and touch: controls keep their metrics and grow their tap areas, invisibly, to 44px: the nav's links and action, the hero's action, the pillars, the control under the pass. No system tap flash. The page reaches the screen's edges and keeps its content clear of the notch and the home bar. The scroll map shows only to a fine pointer. The work's pictures reach a phone as 480px copies.
