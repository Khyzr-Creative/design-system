Voice

How khyzr sounds and how its copy is written — the traits, the moves, the mechanics, the words, and the name. Every quoted example is real copy from the homepage.

Four traits

Plain, confident, unhurried. khyzr is an ally, not a vendor; it says the quiet part ("No pitch —"); it is concrete over abstract; it speaks with calm authority. First person "we", direct "you". No exclamation marks, no hype adjectives, no emoji — ever. The voice was locked on 2026-07-09.

Calm, senior, direct

Short declaratives. No exclamation marks, anywhere. The confidence is in what's left out.

"The right answer isn't always comfortable. We tell you what we see, not what you want to hear."

We, alongside you

khyzr speaks as "we"; the reader is "you" or "your team". Always beside, never above.

"And we come in alongside your people — never over them."

Complete, don't replace

The core frame: the gap, the missing part, one team that fills it. Never "transformation".

"Completing what's missing, not replacing what works." · "Let's build the missing part."

Restraint is positioning

Selectivity lowers pressure and raises value. khyzr can say no — and says so.

"We take on a few partners at a time, so we can go deep." · "We only say yes when we can truly become part of what you're building."

How sentences land

The signature moves.

  • The turn. The sentence ends on a reversal: "…real leverage, not hype." · "…felt as relief, not ceremony." · "…what we see, not what you want to hear." One turn per paragraph, at most.
  • The em-dash pivot. Abstract first, concrete after the dash: "Direction when the path isn't clear — positioning, growth plans, and the hard calls that set everything else in motion."
  • The callback. "…stronger than we found them" echoes from the people band to the name story. One phrase may recur per page — an echo, not a chorus.
  • The reassure. Every big CTA gets a stakes-lowering line beneath it: "A short conversation to see if we're the right fit." or "We only say yes when…". Ask, then soften.
  • Long–short rhythm. A flowing sentence, then a clipped one: "It's never just one thing." The short sentence carries the weight.
  • Two-beat headlines. Period-terminated pairs: "Every discipline. One team." · "Strive forward. Thrive together." The second beat gets the green word.
  • Numbered eyebrows. Section eyebrows are numbered and set in the technical register: "01 · What we do".

Rules of the road

  • CTAs. The primary CTA is always "Book a conversation" — never "Get started", "Learn more", "Contact us" or "Book a demo". Text links are verb-first and carry the arrow: "See the work →". On screen the arrow is the arrow-right icon, not a typed character (see Icons).
  • Casing. Sentence case everywhere, headlines included. Headlines end with periods when they're sentences. Uppercase exists only in the technical register's labels and the hero word.
  • Punctuation. Em dashes and semicolons do the pivoting. No exclamation marks. No emoji. No ellipses. Middle dots (·) join taxonomy labels: "Finance · Accounting".
  • Labels and numerals. Set in the technical register (font-tech), uppercase and tracked. Disciplines are numbered 01–06. Lanes read like a taxonomy, not a tagline: "Healthcare AI", "Brand · Marketing · Design".
  • Claims. Confidence without superlatives about ourselves. We describe what we do and let restraint carry the status — the copy never begs.
  • Taglines. "Everything starts here." opens; "Strive forward. Thrive together." closes, and signs the © line. Don't coin new taglines per page.

The name

Al-Khidr, "the Green One": الخضر

The name is told once per surface, with its story: the sage people sought when the path wasn't clear — an advisor, not a ruler. He came in, guided, and moved on.

  • The Arabic name appears at most once per surface, and only in that moment, under the kicker "The name". It is set in Noto Naskh Arabic.
  • Never as decoration, never sprinkled through copy.
  • Browser and app chrome are exempt.
  • Elsewhere, the company is simply khyzr — always lowercase, and so are its products: khyzr.ai, khyzr.studio. That holds in copy, titles, meta, alt and aria text; code identifiers and file names keep their own spelling. Never "Khyzr", never "khyzr Consulting Group".
