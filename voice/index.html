Voice

How khyzr sounds and how its copy is written: the personality, the eight rules, the mechanics, the words, and the name.

Personality

Approachable. Transparent. Dependable. The brand looks enterprise-formal; the person you meet in the writing is human. First person "we", direct "you". No exclamation marks, no hype adjectives, no emoji, ever.

The eight rules

  1. Approachable, transparent, dependable, in every sentence.
  2. Talk like a human. Plain, conversational sentences. No copywriter punctuation: no em-dash constructions, no colon-heavy headlines.
  3. Empathetic, never accusatory. The buyer's past choices were reasonable.
  4. Concrete over metaphor. Say what actually happens: "the work comes back in separate pieces from separate teams," not "silos."
  5. Calm and assured, never scrappy or underdog. khyzr has reach; write like it.
  6. Forward-motion language. The brand moves things; sentences should too.
  7. Never frame the relationship as ending. No "when we're done," no "we leave you with." Permanence is earned, not declared, so we never declare it either; we just never write the goodbye.
  8. Candor as integrity. We tell you what we see, not what you want to hear.

How sentences land

The signature moves.

  • The turn. The sentence ends on a reversal: "…what we see, not what you want to hear." One turn per paragraph, at most.
  • The callback. One phrase may recur per page, as an echo, not a chorus.
  • The reassure. Every big CTA gets a stakes-lowering line beneath it: "A short conversation to see if we're the right fit." Ask, then soften.
  • Long–short rhythm. A flowing sentence, then a clipped one: "It's never just one thing." The short sentence carries the weight.
  • Two-beat headlines. Period-terminated pairs: "Every discipline. One team." · "Strive Forward. Thrive Together." The second beat gets the green word.
  • Numbered eyebrows. Section eyebrows are numbered and set in the technical register: "01 · What we do".

Rules of the road

  • CTAs. The primary CTA is always "Book a conversation", never "Get started", "Learn more", "Contact us" or "Book a demo". Text links are verb-first and carry the arrow: "See the work →". On screen the arrow is the arrow-right icon, not a typed character (see Icons).
  • Casing. Sentence case everywhere, headlines included; the tagline keeps its own casing. Headlines end with periods when they're sentences. Uppercase exists only in the technical register's labels and the hero word.
  • Punctuation. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no ellipses, and none of the copywriter's em-dash constructions. Middle dots (·) join taxonomy labels: "Finance · Accounting".
  • Labels and numerals. Set in the technical register (font-tech), uppercase and tracked, never a monospace. Disciplines are numbered 01–06. Lanes read like a taxonomy, not a tagline: "Healthcare AI", "Brand · Marketing · Design".
  • Claims. Confidence without superlatives about ourselves. We describe what we do and let restraint carry the status; the copy never begs.
  • Taglines. "Everything starts here." opens; "Strive Forward. Thrive Together.", the carried verbal asset, closes and signs the © line. Don't coin new taglines per page.

Vocabulary

  • "Alongside your team", not "inside yours," not "for you."
  • Completion is the client's felt outcome, never outbound copy. We never tell a client their business is incomplete without us.
  • "The everything company" is internal shorthand and qualified-room sales language; on public surfaces the promise is the whole board, not "anything for anyone."

The name

Al-Khidr, "the Green One": الخضر

The name is told once per surface, with its story: the sage people sought when the path wasn't clear — an advisor, not a ruler. He came in, guided, and moved on.

  • The Arabic name appears at most once per surface, and only in that moment, under the kicker "The name". It is set in Noto Naskh Arabic.
  • Never as decoration, never sprinkled through copy.
  • Browser and app chrome are exempt.
  • Elsewhere, the company is simply khyzr — always lowercase, and so are its products: khyzr.ai, khyzr.studio. That holds in copy, titles, meta, alt and aria text; code identifiers and file names keep their own spelling. Never "Khyzr", never "khyzr Consulting Group".
