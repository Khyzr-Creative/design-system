Design system · Foundations

Tokens

Every value by its token name. Color, type, space, shape, depth, and motion are set once, in five files, and everything else reads them.

The files

The tokens are the values. They live in design-system/tokens/: colors.css, typography.css, spacing.css, motion.css, and fonts.css, which loads the faces.

  • Start from the tokens. design-system/styles.css is the one entry. It imports every token file. Link it and every value on this site is yours.
  • One name, one meaning. A token is declared once. The build stops if a name is declared twice.
  • A prefix says what a token is. text- is a size, fg- a text color, leading- a line height, tracking- a letter spacing, space- a distance, radius- a corner, shadow- a shadow, z- a layer, ease- a curve, dur- a clock.
  • A role points at a color. link, accent, error, and the other roles hold no value of their own. Change the color and the role follows.
  • Where a page and a token disagree, the token is right.

Other formats

The same tokens are published as JSON for tools that cannot read CSS.

FileShape
khyzr-tokens.jsonA plain list: each token with its name, file, group, type, and value, and what it points at when it is a role
khyzr-tokens.dtcg.jsonThe W3C Design Tokens format, for tools that import it
  • Both are written from the CSS files when this site is built. The CSS is the source.
  • A role keeps its pointer: link is written as a reference to green-500, and not as a second copy of its value.
  • A fluid size is a clamp() in the CSS. The W3C format has no fluid size, so there it carries its upper bound, with the whole expression beside it.
  • Tracking is in em, which that format does not hold. It is written as a number, with its unit beside it.

Color

Every color the system names, by group, with one green family behind most of them.

Brand core

  • khyzr-green is the primary green. It is safe for body text on paper as it stands.
  • forest is the deep green for hover, pressed states, and deep fills. deep-forest is the deepest solid. mint is the light sage.

Neutrals

The canvas, the surfaces, and the text grays.

  • paper is the page. surface carries cards. surface-2 carries a section set apart from its white neighbors, a resting card, and a form plate.
  • hairline draws borders. hairline-strong is the same border under the pointer.
  • faint is for rules and large type, muted for secondary text, captions, and labels, body for reading, ink for headings.
  • The text grays keep a green cast. The canvas, surface-2, and the hairlines are neutral.
  • pitch is a surface: dark sections and the gradient's anchor. It is never a text color.

Data-viz roles

The cast of a khyzr chart. Its grammar is on the Data page.

  • viz-lead is series 1, always. A line strokes viz-lead-deep over a viz-lead fill.
  • viz-2 is the second series: a comparison, last year, the benchmark.
  • viz-3 is the third series, and a comparison ghost.
  • viz-4 is the fourth series and "everything else". Past four categories, regroup the data.
  • signal is the one flag: a negative, an annotation. Never a series, never chrome. Small text and text on a tint take signal-text. signal-tint is the wash behind a signal chip.
  • A bar rounds at 5px to 6px. The fill under a line fades from 18% to nothing.
  • On green-900, viz-2 sinks, at 2.3:1. There the series run viz-lead, viz-3, viz-4.
  • green-500, green-700, and green-800 make a ramp for one total split by category. They are never competing series.

Gradient

  • gradient-hero runs from pitch through green-900, forest, leaf, mint, and green-100 to paper, blended in OKLab.
  • gradient-hero-srgb is the same ramp for a renderer without OKLab. State it first and the OKLab one second, so a browser takes the best it can draw.
  • The ramp is used whole, in either direction. No new color is taken from the middle of it.

Floors in force

  • Reading text is body or muted, never faint. Captions and labels are muted. faint holds 3.2:1 on white, under the floor for small text, so it is for rules and large type.
  • green-700 is there for emphatic green text.
  • marigold never sets text. Its twin marigold-text does.
  • terracotta sets chart marks, flags, and large text. Text takes terracotta-text.
  • cobalt is the only secondary that is safe for text.
  • viz-4 is a chart shape beside a labeled value, never text.
  • On green-900: on-dark-heading for headings, on-dark-soft and on-dark-body for reading, on-dark-muted for labels and legal, green-300 for the accent word.
  • muted and cobalt never sit on green-900. They sink.
  • On green-100, text is deep-forest, khyzr-green, or ink.
  • A signal chip sets signal-text on signal-tint, bold or large only.
