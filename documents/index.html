Documents

How khyzr paper reads. Two kinds of document, one anatomy. Start from a template, and never restyle from scratch.

Two kinds of document.

The documentWhite canvas, wordmark header, one flowing column
The brand bookA deep green cover with the mesh, paginated, designed page by page

The template files are not published on this site.

Every khyzr document is one of two kinds, and each has its template.

  • The document. Grounded and informative: a letter, a memo, a proposal, a report, a guide. A white page, the wordmark in the header, and one flowing column of text. It prints on whatever paper the reader has.
  • The brand book. Paginated, and designed page by page: a guide with a cover, a flagship PDF. Full-bleed color, a cover, a dark closing page.
  • The one-page sheet is a brand-book page that stands alone: a dark title band, the sheet, an Ink foot. The one-pager is a document: a white page with one band of the mesh.
  • The two never mix in one file.

One anatomy.

Both kinds are built from the same parts.

PartRule
PageWhite, the same white as the screen. A4, portrait, for anything printed. The khyzr brand book itself is a landscape page, the shape of a slide
MarkThe wordmark, once to a page: top left in the header, and once on the cover
TypeOutfit throughout, with Manrope for the small labels. The sizes are on the Type page
LabelOne small label may name the kind of document: "Memo". No label sits over a heading
FolioA hairline, then a green dot. The document's name at the left, the page number at the right
ClosingA brand book ends on an Ink back cover, with the white badge and the tagline. A document ends where its text ends

Green leads, lightly.

On paper as on screen, the page is white, the type is ink, and green is small: one phrase in a title, a list's numerals, the folio's dot.

  • The secondaries work inside the content: cobalt informs, marigold highlights, steel quiets, terracotta signals.
  • Use one as a light wash behind a note, a colored numeral, a chip, or a rule. Never as a competing field.
  • When a secondary carries words, it takes its darker twin.

Backgrounds, pictures, and icons on paper.

Most documents need none of them. Restraint is the default.

  • Backgrounds. A cover takes the mesh, or the gradient. Inside, a background is a band or a panel, never under reading text, and never a whole page. See Backgrounds.
  • Photographs. Honest documentary, with rounded corners or full bleed, and never beside a background. See Imagery.
  • Icons. Solar only. A line icon beside text, in ink. A two-tone icon at the head of a card or a section, one at a time. See Icons.
  • Marks shown as examples sit on tiles: white with a hairline, the deep green, or Pitch.

Paper does not move.

A document is still. Motion belongs to screens.

  • A document flows: one column, split into pages when it prints, so it fits the reader's paper.
  • A brand book is a fixed set of pages. Each is one full sheet, and what misses the page is cut off.
  • Print, decks, and collateral do not animate.
  • Finished documents leave as PDF, named in plain words for what they are. See Templates and files.
