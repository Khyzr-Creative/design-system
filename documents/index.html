13 · Documents

Documents

How khyzr paper reads — two document classes, one anatomy. Duplicate a template; never restyle from scratch.

Two classes

Every khyzr document is one of two kinds, and each has its template.

The documentWhite canvas, wordmark header, one flowing column
The brand bookDeep-green cover plate, paginated, designed page by page

The template files are not published on this site.

  • The document — grounded, elegant, informative: guides, references, how-tos, memos. A white canvas, the wordmark in the header, and one flowing text column composed from the standard blocks. It prints on whatever paper the reader has.
  • The brand book — spectacular, paginated, designed page by page: the Quick Guide, flagship PDFs, anything with a cover. A deep-green cover plate with a pattern column and a hero word, ghost chapter numerals, one dark statement interlude, and an Ink back cover with contact cards.

Shared anatomy

Both classes are built from the same parts.

PartRule
CanvasPure White — the same canvas as the web. Dark pages use green-900; the back cover is always Ink.
PageA4 or Letter, portrait (the employee Drive prints A4). Brand-book pages take 56–64px padding and full-bleed color. Documents take 0.85in margins and flow.
Type scaleEyebrow: Manrope 600, 12px, .16em, uppercase, green. Title: Outfit at fw-display (600), 40–46px, −.027em. Section heads: Outfit 600, 21–24px. Body: Outfit 14–16px at 1.6. Folios and labels: Manrope 600, 11px, .10em, uppercase. One Instrument Serif italic turn per page, at most.
BlocksWhite cards (radius-m, hairline, no shadows), definition rows split by hairlines, numbered rules, stat tiles on surface-2, and notes washed in a secondary at a 5–10% tint.
FolioA hairline, then a green dot; the document name at left, the page count or contact at right, in Manrope at 11px. Covers and back covers carry the meta grid instead: RELEASE / DATE / QUESTIONS.
LogosThe wordmark in the header or at the cover's top left, at least 90px wide; the white badge on the back cover, which is Ink. Never compose lockups by hand. Marks sit on White, Ink or the deep green only.

Color in documents

Green leads every page: the eyebrows, the rules, the folio dot. The secondaries play across the content — cobalt informs, marigold warms and highlights, steel quiets, terracotta signals.

  • Use the secondaries as washes (a 5–10% tint with a matching border), colored numerals, chips and section rules.
  • They never become competing fields.
  • When a secondary carries copy, it sets in its AA text twin.
  • Ghost chapter numerals sit in green-100 at 55% opacity.

Pattern, imagery, icons

  • Pattern — bands, columns and panels only; the cover column is the signature print use. At most two per page, never behind body text, never a full page.
  • Imagery — honest documentary at radius-m, never sharing a band with the pattern. Most documents need no photos: restraint is the default.
  • Icons — Solar only. Linear beside text, in ink; Line Duotone at 32px and up, in forest — one per card or section, never more than two tones.

Files and releases

  • Exported PDFs are named Khyzr <document> (verdant-N).pdf, carrying the brand-kit release: Khyzr Quick Guide (verdant-2).pdf. Nothing is edited downstream.
  • Every document carries its release — in the header, the cover meta or the back cover.
  • Questions route to cisco@khyzr.com.
