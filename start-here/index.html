The khyzr brand guidelines. What they are, who they are for, the few rules that matter most, and where the files are.

What this is.

These are khyzr's brand guidelines: how the brand looks and sounds, wherever it appears. They cover khyzr and its two products, khyzr.ai and khyzr.studio.

  • Read a chapter when you need it. Each one is short.
  • Start from a template wherever one exists. It already follows every rule here.
  • If these pages do not answer your question, ask.

Who it is for.

Everyone who makes something that carries the khyzr name.

  • Everyone at khyzr. The chapters, from The brand to In use.
  • Partners and agencies. The same chapters, and the files in the brand kit.
  • Designers and printers. The appendix: color values, logo files, type in each tool.
  • People building a khyzr website. The design system, behind the other door.

The rules that matter most.

If you read nothing else, keep these.

  1. khyzr is lowercase. Always, and so are khyzr.ai and khyzr.studio.
  2. Use the logo files. Never redraw, recolor, or rebuild a mark.
  3. White first, ink for type, green last. Green is small, so that it is always the point.
  4. One typeface. Outfit sets everything. Headlines are sentences that end with a period.
  5. White is always right. The mesh is the default background. The logo pattern is rare.
  6. Real photographs of real work. khyzr does not illustrate, and never generates people.
  7. Credit, never co-branding. Another company is named in words, not joined to khyzr's mark.

Where the files are.

One Drive for everyone at khyzr, and one kit for everyone else.

  • The khyzr brand Drive. For everyone at khyzr: logos, templates, the brand book, fonts and color, backgrounds, photos, and icons, and a folder for each product.
  • The brand kit. The same files as a download, for everyone outside khyzr: partners, agencies, and printers. Ask for it.
  • The brand site. The guidelines in full, and the design system for builders, at khyzr-creative.github.io/design-system.
  • The whole list, folder by folder, is in Templates and files.
