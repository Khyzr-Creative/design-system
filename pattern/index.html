08 · Expression

Pattern

Paper cutouts in the brand greens — generative, seeded, always deliberate. This is the website's texture, and it dresses sections and elements, never whole pages.

The asset

Two themes, one component. The pattern is a single element, <khyzr-pattern>, from khyzr-pattern.js in the design system's pattern assets. Production uses this asset. It was adopted as the official website texture on 2026-07-13.

The element is static, deterministic and sized by its container. Any number of instances share one WebGL context. Without WebGL it paints a quiet solid fill in its theme's canvas color.

theme="dark"

theme="light"

  • Dark is the default and its look is locked: the green-900 canvas, with the palette shapes composited at 10% and pre-flattened. Text is safe on it as it is.
  • Light is grayscale, at 50% strength on a #E9E9E9 canvas. Text on it stays at body or darker, and long-form copy needs a solid panel.
  • Seeded means the same composition on every load.

Attributes

AttributeWhat it does
themedark (the default) or light
seedany number; sets a deterministic composition (default 7)
scalecutout size multiplier, sensible from 0.5 to 2 (default 1)
animatewhen present, a slow ambient drift — moved by CSS transform only, with no re-rendering, and off automatically under reduced motion

Place the element absolutely, filling a positioned container that clips its overflow, and set the content above it.

<khyzr-pattern theme="dark" seed="7" scale="1">

Do and don't

DoDon't
Hero bands, CTA cards, stat strips, dividers, footer capsFull-page backgrounds — it's seasoning, not the plate
Card media slots and empty statesLong-form body copy on the light theme without a solid panel
Set text directly on the dark theme — shapes are 10% tints, quiet by designRecoloring, gradients on top, or mixing themes in one section
Pick a seed per surface and keep it — compositions are part of the designMore than two pattern surfaces in a viewport
  • Sections, cards, media slots and dividers — never the page background.
  • Never recolor. The themes are the only variations.
  • A photo and the pattern never share a band.

Hero band

The dark hero band: dark theme, seed 7, scale 1.

  • No scrim needed — the copy sits straight on the pattern.
  • The headline is Outfit at the display weight (fw-display), in on-dark-heading.
  • The calls to action are the white button and the hero ghost button from Components.

Bands and dividers

Stat strip

  • Dark theme, seed 33, scale 0.8 — a finer field under small type.
  • Numbers in Outfit 600 at 46px.
  • One accent figure at most, in green-300.

Section divider

  • Dark theme, seed 5, scale 0.6, 110px high.
  • Label only — a divider never carries body copy.

Cards

Pattern as card media, shown on the service cards.

  • The media cap is 150px high: dark theme, seeds 11, 23 and 31, scale 0.7.
  • One seed per card, fixed. Siblings must differ.
  • The card body stays on solid white.

Light theme

CTA card

  • Light theme, seed 21, scale 0.85, radius radius-l.
  • Short copy only on the light pattern — headings, and ink text at body or darker.

Pull quote

  • Light theme, seed 40, scale 0.9.
  • Instrument Serif italic is the page's one serif accent (the Voice rule).

Footer cap

The closing band.

  • Dark theme, seed 3, scale 1.2 — a calmer, larger field.
  • The on-dark wordmark at 34px high.
  • The legal row in on-dark-faint.
