Design system · As built

khyzr.ai as built

The khyzr.ai site as it stands. Where its color lives, its type, space, and corners, its depth and its motion, the hero, every section in page order, and the site-wide pieces. The product's mark and color are its brand chapter, and the frame it shares with khyzr.studio is written once.

Shared and its own

  • With khyzr.studio: the frame, the seams, the type scale, the nav, the closing, the colophon, focus, the button skeletons, the radius ladder, the scroll map, the eased wheel, and the scroll entrance, shared by name and structure. See The product frame.
  • Its own: color, the hero, the engines, every section's content, and the timings of its motion.
  • Held in the system: every value on this page is in tokens/products/khyzr-ai.css, a checked copy that states the commit it was read at.

Where color lives

The ramp, its four stops, and what each means are on the product's brand chapter, khyzr.ai.

  • How much: the page is about four fifths pure black. Near-black plates and windows take 16 to 17%, gray and light ink about 2%, and color about 1%. The hero's held moment is 93% black and 2% color.
  • Tinted neutrals: every neutral leans to the ramp's hue at a very low chroma, so the grays read faintly blue.
  • In the console the four stops have jobs: a2 for actuals and selection (cw-actual), a1 for plans and forecasts (cw-plan), a3 for focus and highlight (cw-focus), a4 for peaks and live marks (cw-live). Two quiet grays carry what is not the point.
  • Where the ramp lives: focus, selection and the caret, the nav underline on hover, the primary button's hover, the hero's rings and the light on its action, Setup's numerals, the lit edges of the product windows and of the account in Security, the lit ends of hairlines, the closing plate's rim, the scroll map's marker, and the light behind the console.
  • Behind the console stands light, not a band: a halo in the page's blues, a glint along the window's top rim, and motes in the page's inks, now and then in an agent's color.
  • The window surfaces: both product windows set their own, darker than the page's raised tone: a body at 8% lightness (win-ground, #010202) and chrome at 13% (win-chrome, #060809). The values of the same names on the root are never seen.
  • Status: ok green, warn amber, danger red, and info, which reads a2 on the page and a1 in the console. The console's set is a shade quieter. A status pill is its color at 14% over the ground.
  • The six agents each carry an identity pair, a light value and a deep one (ag-*), in the console and in Security.
  • The seven capabilities each carry a ramp of four stops, deep to light (k1 to k4). The selected one colors its icon, its rail segment, the progress rule, the tab underline, and the chat backdrop.
  • The traffic lights on a window's title bar are the familiar three of a desktop window: #FF5F57, #FEBC2E, #28C840.
  • Contrast on black: ink 17.1:1; ink2 6.0:1, and 5.6:1 on the closing plate; the ramp 6.2, 8.3, 11.2, and 15.0:1 from a1 to a4. hair2 holds 1.5:1 and is decoration only.

The radius ladder

  • One family, three weights, and these sizes only on the page itself.
  • The product windows keep smaller sizes of their own: the console and its crops read a UI ramp of six, from 9 to 20px (ui-fs-tick to ui-fs-big), and the chat window its own steps.
  • The Arabic name in the colophon is the one element outside Outfit: Noto Naskh Arabic, as Type sets it.
  • Every corner on the page reads a step of the radius ladder. The seven names and values are the same on khyzr.studio.
  • Corners in use: the hero's action 4px; every other button 6px; what sits inside a window 8px; Security's plates 12px; the windows, the panels, and the crops 16px. Lines and the bands that span the screen stay square.
  • Two things sit off the ladder by design: the chat question's pill, whose radius is a ratio of the drawn object (cv-q-r), and an icon's tile, which is artwork.

Depth and light

On black, light does the lifting. Shadow only seats what is already lit.

  • Frost: a panel is rgba(4,6,10,.42) over a 22px blur with an edge of white at 8.5% and a highlight of white at 7.5% inside its top edge. Inside the two product windows it is denser: .62, a 25px blur, an edge of white at 5%. A second, denser frost carries what floats over a panel: popovers, tooltips, and callouts.
  • A lifted panel takes a deep, tight shadow: 0 28px 60px -28px at black 90% and 0 2px 4px at black 35%. The windows take 0 40px 90px -30px at black 76% and 0 3px 8px at black 32%.
  • The lit ring: the console window, its three crops, and the chat window wear one 1px ring, a gradient at 135°: white at 22% at the top left, falling to white at 7%, then hair2, then a3 at 24% at the bottom right. It is the family likeness of every frame on the page.
  • The halo behind the console is a2, a3, and a1 as three soft lights under an elliptical mask.
  • Glow is scoped to seven named places: the selected capability's color, its rail's glow, the chat backdrop's marks, the hero's action, the agents' colors in the console, the same in Security, and the light behind the console. Nothing else on the page glows.
  • Text shadow is the hero's alone: a neutral offset shadow, 0 4px 4px at black 42% under the headline and 25% under the lede. Never a halo, never colored.
  • Grain: one fixed layer over the whole page, at 12% in overlay.
  • Hairlines are 1px in hair or hair2. Borders define plates; rules sit between rows, under the bar, inside tables, and at the colophon's foot.

Motion

  • ease-out carries every entrance and hover; ease-in-out is kept for what travels on screen; ease-long carries the closing seal's bloom. Nothing eases in and nothing overshoots.
  • Clocks: 160ms is the page's default and 240ms its second. A press scales to .97 in 160ms; color changes take 160ms, edges 180ms, fills 120ms. Nothing grows under the pointer.
  • The entrance: type rises by the line over 700ms, 70ms apart; blocks rise 12 to 28px over 700 to 1000ms; a sequence arrives 140ms apart. Each section's own picture arrives once, through its content.
  • What runs on: the hero's rings, the closing's band, the chat's backdrop, the console's motes and underlight, the light on the hero's action, and the rim. Each pauses off screen.
  • Reduced motion: every arrival stands finished and nothing travels; the hero's rings hold one moment; the load is opacity only.

The hero

One luminous element, the rings, behind one centered lockup.

  • The plate: the screen's width and 82vh tall, with 64px above and below, on the black ground. At 640px and under it is 70vh, with 56px above and 48px below; on a phone on its side it takes its content's height.
  • The rings: expanding rings, read once per cell and written in the page's own glyph marks, in the ramp. Their origin stays on the headline while the page scrolls: the canvas takes no parallax.
  • The run-on: the canvas stands taller than the plate. Past the hero's foot the marks thin steadily (tildes, stars, dots, nothing), so only dots reach the console's heading.
  • Under the pointer: a ripple, and a lens that bows the field around the pointer, constant while the pointer rests on the canvas. Neither runs under reduced motion.
  • The lockup: one centered column: the headline in fs-h1 at a 16ch measure, the lede at 48ch in full ink, and one action, 40px apart. The root's own measures, 12ch and 34ch, are overridden here.
  • The action: the hero's own button on the shared skeleton, 40px tall on r-xs: a conic light turning on its border, a speckle, a sheen, and a prompt glyph before its label, a chevron and an underscore on a 16 grid. It is lit in a3 and a4, with a glow under the label on hover and focus only.
  • The load: the nav fades in from 120ms; the headline's two lines rise from 150ms, 700ms each and 70ms apart; the lede, then the action, fade in and lift 8px at 420 and 520ms; the rings come up from 250ms over 1.2s.
  • Reduced motion: the load is opacity only, in 300ms. The rings hold one moment: a single ring around the headline, the lede, and the action, clear of all three. The button's light stands still, without its glow.
  • Phone: at 720px and under the headline is set between 34 and 44px. A portrait hero adds a second ring half a cycle behind the first, its clear center a rounded rectangle 12px clear of the copy. The bar's action waits while the hero's is on screen.

The page

Eight blocks in order: the nav, the hero, the console, the capabilities, setup, security, the closing, the colophon. Every section from the console to the closing opens on a major seam; the closing sits one minor seam above the footer. No rule divides one section from the next.

  • The section head: the console, the capabilities, setup, and security open on one shape: a centered statement in fs-h2 in ink over a continuation in fs-cont in ink2, one column, no rule. The closing's head is the one featured tier, fs-feat, with no continuation.
  • One family of frames: the console window, its three crops, and the chat window wear the same continuous lit ring, on r-xl.
  • The action: "Get started", three times: in the bar, in the hero, and in the closing.

The console

A head, then a real product window, then three crops of it.

  • The window: built in HTML, 16:9, drawn at 1104 × 621 and scaled to the column. Its body is the darkest ground on the page; its title bar and icon rail are lighter chrome, with traffic lights; its panels are frosted. The icon stands whole atop the rail, at 28px.
  • The underlight: a light in the ramp rising from the window's foot. It pauses off screen and is static under reduced motion.
  • Depth: the window stands in a 1800px perspective and tilts toward the pointer, to 3.2° across and 2.4° up and down, easing back to rest as the pointer walks away. No tilt under a coarse pointer or reduced motion. No light follows the pointer.
  • Five screens stand behind the rail, each with its tabs in the title bar. A switch takes 110ms out and 220ms in, with a 4px rise; the window's height stays pinned, so nothing under it moves.
  • What a panel holds: one idea. No third line of small type under a row. A legend only where nothing else labels the mark. Color only for the exception. An agent's status carries no dot: its identity mark owns the color, and only an alert takes danger.
  • It answers the pointer without moving: panels take a brighter edge; rail buttons light by proximity; the chart has a crosshair with a readout, legend toggles, and a range control, whose change is a soft cut, 100ms out and 220ms in. A frosted popover, a 380px sheet that slides over, an ask bar with suggestions, and a command palette from the search glyph, the K tile, or the keyboard. Tabs, rail, and cells are real buttons. A callout above the window leaves for good on the first rail click.
  • Behind it: the halo, the glint, and the motes. The motes drift in toward the window and are gone before they reach it; they draw only while the window is on screen, and stand as one still scatter under reduced motion.
  • Entrance: the window rises 28px and settles from 98.5% over 1s; the halo follows from 320ms over 1.4s, the motes from 700ms over 1.8s. 48px of room stands above the window.
  • The crops: three plates one major seam under the window, on tri-cols with sp-3 between them. Each is a fixed 346:240 on r-xl: an excerpt of one screen behind the rail, scaled to fit. Each plays its own small arrival once, at a third on screen. The captions sit beside their plates at 900px and under, above them at 640px and under.
  • Touch: a touch screen at 1100px and under shows the crops, not the window.

The capabilities

A head, then a stepper list of seven rows beside one chat window, centered on each other.

  • A row: an 18px drawn icon and a title. The selected row adds a description, which fades in while the row's content rides up, so no row resizes. Rows are ruled between, with a 1px rail at the left.
  • States: resting rows are ink2. Hover answers on the title only, to ink. The icon never reacts to the pointer, only to selection: selected, it cross-fades in 200ms from gray to its capability's gradient.
  • The lit segment: 3px of the rail beside the selected row, in the same gradient with a soft glow in its own colors. It moves between rows in 260ms on ease-in-out and eases its colors in 240ms. A 1px rule under the selected row draws the autoplay clock.
  • Autoplay advances every 10s while the section is on screen, and stops for good on the first click, key press, or focus in the composer. Arrow keys, Home, and End move the selection: it is a real tab list.
  • The window: opaque, on the console's darkest ground, with a trimmed title bar holding a spark tile and the agent's name with its role after it in the second ink, the wordmark's own two-tone idiom.
  • The thread sits on the composer. The question is the only filled shape: on the right, white at 6%, at most 85% of the thread wide, a pill by cv-q-r. Under it, a trace line with one status ring; the reply, unboxed; one artifact on a titled sunk panel on r-md; one ghost action. The composer is a pill that leads with the symbol.
  • A switch: the answered exchange lifts away as it fades, 10px in 140ms, so the window never stands empty; the question and its trace rise in 220ms, the ring pulses, the reply rises in 240ms, and the artifact follows 90ms behind. The first exchange plays when the window comes on screen.
  • Size: side by side, a 380px list and a 700px window stand 72px apart, and the pair takes the taller one's height: the list's from 1100px, so the window grows to it. There the exchange reads a larger step of its own type, so it fills the window.
  • The backdrop: a faint glyph field behind the window, in the selected capability's colors, each mark with a faint halo in its own color, fading to nothing a little past the window's edge. Static under reduced motion.
  • 960px and under: the list becomes a tab strip over the window, exactly as wide as it. The selected tab's underline is the rail lying down, gliding from tab to tab; the strip fades only on a side with more to scroll.
  • Touch: the two ask fields are read-only under a coarse pointer.

Setup

A head, then three steps in a row on tri-cols.

  • The numerals: 1, 2, and 3, drawn in the page's glyph marks on a 22 × 15 grid, in the ramp, each on a floor of dots. Cells are 12px at 1440px, 10px from 1101 to 1239px, 8px at 1100px and under, and 7px at 400px and under.
  • A step: its numeral at the left of its words at every width; the step named in one word in fs-feat, over one line of body. It is the page's one numbered sequence, because it is a real one.
  • Arrival: each numeral is drawn in one sweep, once, when the list is 35% on screen or fills 40% of the screen.
  • Hover: pointing at a step replays its numeral's sweep while the other two quiet to .4, in 240ms. The replay never runs under reduced motion; the quieting does.
  • Narrower: at 1100px and under the list is centered as a block; at 640px and under the step names drop to fs-cont.

Security

A head, then two plates side by side on one grid, the usual way and ours, then three promises.

  • The usual way is gray and takes no lift.
  • Ours is lifted, on r-lg. A dashed line runs around four systems; inside it stands the account, with a lit edge running a4 at the top left to a1 at the bottom right, and a dot grid holding the six agents in their identity colors. Each system's link ends on a port on the account's edge; its pulse wears the color of the agent that picks it up, and that agent's edge brightens.
  • The frame: one line of the page's glyph tildes just inside our plate's edge, a4 at the top left to a1 at the bottom right, with one slow wave of brightness traveling around it.
  • The names: our plate's title sets .ai in a1. The icon stands whole at 24px on the edge of the account's label.
  • The promises: three, on the three-up grid, each under a hairline lit at its left end.
  • Arrival: the links run out 70ms apart, each over 720ms on ease-in-out, and the pulses follow. Motion runs only on screen and never under reduced motion; without script the picture stands finished.
  • Narrower: the plates stack from 1100px and the promises from 960px; each picture turns top to bottom at 720px; the section stacks at 640px, and the agents' chips tighten at 360px.

The closing

A plate the screen's width on g-band, square, one minor seam above the footer.

  • The band: across the plate's top, 140px tall, inside the plate, so band and plate read as one piece: a field of the page's glyph marks that ripples under the pointer. A swipe that starts on it scrolls the page.
  • The seal: the symbol bare on the band's hairline, 40px, or 32px at 720px and under. The hairline parts for it and the band's marks clear around it. It blooms once on arrival, pair by pair from each pair's corner (back, middle, front), 140ms apart, 900ms each on ease-long, and the seam opens with it.
  • The column: one, centered: the line in fs-feat, the lede, a 64px rule, one primary action. sp-2 from line to lede; the rule stands centered with sp-3 either side.
  • The rim: one point of light in the ramp, lapping the plate's edge once in 14s.
  • Without script the band is absent and the seal sits whole on the plate's top edge.

The colophon

  • Columns: the brand and a line, then navigate, legal, and contact, their labels real headings. It stacks at 640px and under.
  • The last line: the copyright with khyzr's Arabic name, then the build date, then the symbol bare, as the page's end mark.
  • Touch: the links stand in 36px rows.

Buttons

  • Primary: an ink fill with black words on r-sm. Under the pointer the fill turns a3; the words stay black.
  • Ghost: no fill, ink words, and a hair2 border. The bar's action is this one, at the small size; under the pointer its border warms toward a2.
  • The hero's action is its own, described with the hero.
  • Every button is a small-cornered rectangle. A press scales to .97.

Icons

The site draws its own set. It does not use Solar, khyzr's icon family.

  • One drawing language: an 18 or 24 grid, a 1.5 stroke, round caps and joins, open outlines, no fills.
  • The set: seven capability icons on the 18 grid; six agent identity marks on the 24 grid, each stroked with its agent's gradient; five icons on the console's rail; and a few of the interface's own: a spark tile, a search glyph, a K tile, the prompt glyph on the hero's action.
  • Sizes: 18px in rows, 24px in chips and on the rail, 16px in the hero's action.
  • States: a capability's icon is gray at rest and takes color only with selection, never with the pointer. An agent's mark keeps its color always.
  • None of them exists as a file: each is drawn inline in the page.

The glyph marks

The site's one pattern. It is not khyzr's logo pattern, and it stands in its place.

  • Four marks on a grid: a dot, a large dot, a six-point star, and a tilde. Each cell is read once and written as one mark, in the ramp.
  • Where they write: the hero's rings, Setup's three numerals, the closing's band, the frame around Security's second plate, and the field behind the chat window.
  • Cells: 24px in the hero; 12px in the band and the numerals, stepping down to 7px on the smallest phone.
  • Order: as the light grows, a cell reads a dot, a large dot, a star, then a tilde. Where the light is gone, the cell is empty.
  • The stills and the four marks as files are with the product's artwork. See khyzr.ai.

What the site leaves out

The site's own guard. Each line holds across the whole page.

  • No monospace, and no second typeface.
  • No eyebrow above a heading, and no label in capitals.
  • No accent word in a headline, no gradient text, no italic.
  • Numbering only for the one real sequence, Setup's three steps.
  • No dash separator, middle dot, or arrow in an interface string.
  • No glass as decoration: frost only where something stands over something else.
  • No strip of statistics.
  • No photograph, no person, and no illustration. The product's own window is the picture, with one diagram, in Security.
  • No light scheme.

Across the site

  • The scroll map: its dots are each block's own inks: grays for type and frames, the accents where the page is blue, the agents' colors on Security's plate. The marker's rule runs a3 into a2, and a hovered cluster lights in a3.
  • Selection and the caret: selection is a2 at 45%; the caret and the form accent are a2. Where the browser's scrollbar shows, it is hair2 on black.
  • The icon stands in five places, never beside a wordmark: whole on its deep tile atop the console's rail and on Security's account; bare as the closing's seal, as the colophon's end mark, and leading both ask fields. It is drawn in the mark's own colors, not the tokens, and its tile takes no radius from the ladder. The footer keeps the wordmark at its top and the symbol on its last line, never side by side.
  • khyzr on the page: the product's name, and the Arabic name once, in the copyright line. No khyzr logo, no line of endorsement, and no link to khyzr.com or to khyzr.studio.
  • The lost page: one screen with no nav and no colophon: the symbol alone, between 56 and 72px, blooming once on load, over the closing's own column: its head, its lede, one primary action.
  • The legal pages: three, in the homepage's own shell (its nav, its frame, its colophon), with no script. One 64ch measure: a head closed by a hairline, then a heading and its paragraphs for each section, the prose in ink2; the imprint's fields in two columns, one at 640px and under. Links are underlined in hair2 and turn a2 under the pointer. The bar's frost comes on within the first 2px of scroll, where the browser can.
  • The social card: 1200 × 630, the site's own first screen at a pinned moment of the hero's motion: the ring around the headline and the lede, the nav reduced to its wordmark and the action hidden. The wordmark stands 56px in from the top left at 1.4 times its nav size. The icon is never set beside it.
  • Icons in use: the favicon as an SVG with a 32px PNG, and a 180px home-screen icon, carried inside the page itself. No web manifest.
  • One file, no outside request: the fonts travel inside the page. Outfit holds its text until the face is ready, so nothing reflows when it arrives; the Arabic name carries only the glyphs it sets.
  • Without script: nothing is hidden. The load's held states apply only once script has announced itself. The console window stands in the flow, the crops and Security's pictures stand finished, and there are no motes and no closing band.
  • Phone and touch: the bar's row is 60px at 640px and under, over its 1px rule, its frost denser, and shorter still on a phone on its side. Controls take 44px targets without changing size. Hover rules need a hover; no system tap flash; the page's text does not resize itself. The scroll map shows only to a fine pointer.
