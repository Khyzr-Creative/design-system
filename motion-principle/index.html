Motion as a principle

How khyzr moves, as an idea. Things grow in. The curves and the clocks are in the design system.

Things grow in.

khyzr's motion behaves like growth. It is called the Green Trace.

  • An element rises crisp from its own baseline, with a breath of overshoot, and settles.
  • Elements arrive overlapping, one while the last is still moving. Growth, not a queue.
  • Never a flicker, never out of focus.
  • Nothing animates twice. Each thing arrives once, and then it is simply there.

What it looks like.

The idea, in three cases.

  • Type rises from its baseline.
  • Bars grow from theirs, and numbers count up to their value.
  • On a screen that can run it, the mesh drifts slowly behind white type.

Where the brand moves.

Motion belongs to screens. It is a behavior of khyzr's websites, not a layer of the brand.

  • On a website: yes, by the rules of the design system. See Motion.
  • In a deck: no. Slides cut from one to the next, with no transitions and no builds.
  • In print and documents: no. The mesh is a still, and so is everything else.
  • For anyone who asks for less: a reader who has turned motion down gets the finished state at once. Motion is a privilege, never a requirement.
