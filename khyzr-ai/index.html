khyzr.ai

AI agents that run inside the client's own AWS account. khyzr.ai keeps khyzr's type and frame and brings its own colour — one periwinkle-to-aqua ramp on a near-black ground.

Shared and its own

The two product sites are one system with two colours. What they hold in common is a vocabulary, not a design.

  • From khyzr: the mark system. The product wordmark is the corporate wordmark with .ai; the product icon is the corporate symbol in this product's ramp. The corporate rules in Logos still govern the corporate mark.
  • With khyzr.studio: the frame, the type scale, the nav, the closing, the colophon, focus, the button skeletons and the radius ladder, shared by name and structure.
  • Its own: colour, the hero, the engines, and every section's content.
  • Type: Outfit only, at three weights by role — khyzr's one family, without the technical face: the product sites load no Manrope. See Type.

The mark

Product marks follow one set of rules — icon or wordmark, never both; the icon's structure; the tiles and favicons — kept in Logos. This page holds only what is this product's own.

  • On dark: khyzr in the site's off-white, .ai in a1 #7280FC.
  • On light: khyzr in the site's ground #070809; .ai stays a1.
  • One color: the product wordmark in the corporate mono values, black and white.

Ink

Accent ramp

  • Structure: a near-black ground in three steps — base, raised, and the console's framing band — with two hairlines, all faintly tinted toward the accent hue; two ink levels; one hue family as a four-stop ramp. The ramp is a hue sweep, periwinkle to aqua, every stop bright.
  • a1 is the focus ring, the accent field's top rule, and the far end of lit gradients.
  • a2 is selection, the caret, the form accent, the nav underline on hover, and the current stage's numeral.
  • a3 is the hot stop: the accent field's fill, the primary button's hover, a stage rule on hover.
  • a4 appears only inside gradients.
  • Where colour lives: focus, selection and caret, link underlines on hover, the primary button's hover, rims, active and done markers, one active word, the rail segment, the index marker, the tile wash, the ripple, and one full-bleed accent field — flat a3, with the ink flipping to the ground.
  • Strictly monochrome: all type, the nav, buttons at rest, plates and tiles at rest, hairlines, dot grids, tables, the FAQ and the footer. No gradient text; headings are always monochrome.
  • Ratio: about 85% near-black, 3% light and 11% saturated on the hero viewport.
