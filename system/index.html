khyzr

Design system

For builders. Every value by its token, the foundations, motion, the components and forms, the pattern elements, documents in code, and the three sites as built.

Foundations

01TokensEvery value by its token name. Color, type, space, shape, depth, and motion are set once, in five files, and everything else reads them.02FoundationsThe structure every khyzr page stands on. One column and its measures, four corners, hairlines and the few shadows, keyboard focus, three layers, and a spacing scale of six steps.03MotionThe Green Trace. Things grow in. Four curves, seven clocks, and the rules for everything that moves on a khyzr page.

Interface

04ComponentsThe primitives the system builds, and the rules each one carries: the button, the eyebrow, the status chip, the proof figure, and the plate field. A primitive reads the tokens and nothing else. khyzr.com's own navigation, sections, and footer are on its as-built page.05FormsPlate fields. A field is a filled plate with no border at rest, and turns white under a green ring on focus. Soft, roomy, and quiet until you touch it.06The pattern elementsThe two patterns as code and as files. <khyzr-field> draws the mesh and <khyzr-logo-pattern> draws the logo pattern, and every still of either is drawn by the same engine. When to use a background, and where, is the brand's Backgrounds chapter.07Assets in codeThe marks, the icons, the fonts, and the pictures as files in a page: where each lives, which file a job takes, and how an icon takes its color and its size. Which mark and which icon to use is in the brand's Logos and Icons chapters.08Documents for buildersThe two document classes as they are built: one stylesheet, one element, the anatomy of each page, and the blocks a page is composed from. What a khyzr document is, and when to use which class, is the brand's Documents chapter.

As built

09khyzr.com as builtThe record of khyzr.com as it stands: its layout and scale, the values it sets beside the tokens, its marks and pictures, its navigation and sections, the pages beside the homepage, and its floors. The tokens stand. These values are the site's own.10The product frameWhat the khyzr.ai and khyzr.studio sites hold in common. One column, one type scale, one nav, one ladder of corners, one closing, and one way of arriving, shared by name and structure. Each site's own design is on its as-built page.11khyzr.ai as builtThe khyzr.ai site as it stands. Where its color lives, its type, space, and corners, its depth and its motion, the hero, every section in page order, and the site-wide pieces. The product's mark and color are its brand chapter, and the frame it shares with khyzr.studio is written once.12khyzr.studio as builtThe khyzr.studio site as it stands. Where its color lives, its type, space, and corners, its depth and its motion, the hero, every section in page order, and the site-wide pieces. The product's mark and color are its brand chapter, and the frame it shares with khyzr.ai is written once.
