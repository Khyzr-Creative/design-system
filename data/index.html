Data

Cobalt and signal — green carries, terracotta flags. khyzr charts say one thing at a time.

Green carries, terracotta flags

Green is always the point: series 1, the number that matters. Everything else supports — cobalt for the comparison, steel for context, and one terracotta flag when something must be seen. Terracotta is loud on purpose and rare on purpose: never a series, never mistaken for failure. The grammar is the Canopy, adopted on 2026-07-16 and recut on 2026-08-08 to the Verdant standard secondaries.

Grammar — the Canopy

Four rules, no exceptions.

  • Green takes series 1, always. The lead series is khyzr Green; no chart leads with an auxiliary. The greens ramp (500, 700, 800) appears only in categorical splits, never as competing series.
  • Soft, rounded, editorial. Bars round at radius 5–6. Area fills fade from 18% to zero. Axes are single hairline rules. No 3D, no gridironed plots, no shadows-as-data.
  • Outfit numerals, technical labels. Values set in Outfit at 600, labelled at the endpoint on the chart where possible. Axes, legends and captions are Manrope, the technical register. Proof numbers stay display-scale.
  • Charts obey the Green Trace. Bars grow from their baseline on ease-drift; numbers count up over dur-count with the settle pulse. One arrival per chart per page view — nothing loops, nothing animates twice.

Palette — the cast

One lead, cobalt second, steel context, one signal.

  • khyzr Green, viz-lead — series 1, always. Lines stroke Forest, viz-lead-deep, over green-500 fills; endpoint dots and the big numeral go deep.
  • Cobalt, viz-2 — the second series: comparisons, last year, the benchmark. Cobalt is the info secondary, and text-safe on paper.
  • Steel, viz-3 — the third series, and comparison ghosts: last year's dashed line behind this year's green.
  • Steel tint, viz-4 — the fourth series, remainders, "everything else". Past four categories, regroup the data instead.
  • Terracotta, signal — the signal: flags, negatives, annotations. One terracotta moment per chart, never a series, never UI chrome. Small text, and text on tints, uses the twin signal-text; signal-tint is the wash behind signal chips.

Why the signal is loud

The auxiliaries stay cool so green keeps the floor. The signal is exempt — rare earns loud. Terracotta is hotter than anything else on the chart, so one flag reads across the room.

A flag is not a failure

error stays feedback-only and never enters a chart. It is the signal's own text twin: one warm family for flags and failures, told apart by placement rather than shade.

Still banned

  • Bright leaf stays gradient-only.
  • No auxiliary leads, and none appears without green.
  • Links, buttons and focus rings stay green. The signal never touches chrome.

Specimens

The six shapes of a khyzr chart.

  • Trend, this year against last — the green line strokes forest over a fading fill. The comparison is steel, dashed and labelled at its endpoint — no legend box.
  • Grouped bars, one flag — the flagged value swaps its cobalt for terracotta, once per chart. Its label uses the text twin.
  • Split, four categories — one rounded bar. The biggest slice is green; the auxiliaries follow in weight order: cobalt, steel, steel tint. No pies, no donuts.
  • Proof number — a deep forest numeral, a green unit mark and a caption in the technical register. It counts up over dur-count with the settle pulse.
  • Annotation on the line — the annotation kit: a terracotta dot, a dashed riser and a short technical note. The line itself stays green.
  • On dark — on green-900, skip cobalt: it sinks, at 2.3:1. Series run green, steel, steel tint; terracotta stays legible for marks.

Display-scale proof number — deep forest numeral, green unit mark, technical-label caption (Manrope).

Label endpoints on the chart; skip legend boxes where you canConfuse flags with failures — error states live in forms and never enter a chart
