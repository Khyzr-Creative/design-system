Design system · As built

The product frame

What the khyzr.ai and khyzr.studio sites hold in common. One column, one type scale, one nav, one ladder of corners, one closing, and one way of arriving, shared by name and structure. Each site's own design is on its as-built page.

One system, two colors

The two product sites are one system with two colors. What they hold in common is a vocabulary, not a design.

  • Shared by name and value: the column and the seams, the type scale and its roles, the section head, the nav's shape, the focus ring's shape, the button skeletons, the radius ladder, the skeleton of the closing and of the colophon, the scroll map, the eased wheel.
  • Shared by name, each with its own value: the ground's own name, the focus color, the light on the hero's action, the timings of the entrance.
  • Each site's own: color, the hero, the engines, the pictures, every section's content.
  • A shared value changes on both sites in the same change.

Where the values are held

Each site keeps its own tokens, in its own repository. The design system holds a checked copy of each.

  • The copies: tokens/products/khyzr-ai.css and tokens/products/khyzr-studio.css. Each states the commit of the site's main branch it was read at.
  • The site keeps custody. A value changes on the site first and the copy follows. The copy is written by script and never by hand, and a check fails when a site's values have moved on from it.
  • What a copy holds: every custom property the site declares on its root, at every width, and the scopes that carry a role. For khyzr.ai: the hero, the console, the capabilities, and the seven capability ramps. For khyzr.studio: the five pillars' steps of the ramp.
  • The names collide. ink is khyzr's near-black, khyzr.ai's light type color, and khyzr.studio's black ground; hair and focus-ring differ the same way. So each copy stands under its own scope, [data-brand="khyzr-ai"] or [data-brand="khyzr-studio"], and never on the root.
  • An oklch() value carries its sRGB hex beside it in the copy.
  • The specimens on the two as-built pages read each site's main branch when this site is built.

The column and the seams

  • The column: one column 1200px wide (frame), centered on the black ground, with 16 to 24px (pad) between it and the screen's edge once the screen is narrower. No rails and no frame lines.
  • What spans the screen: the nav, the hero, and the closing, each keeping its content on the column. On khyzr.studio the membership band spans the screen too, and the work's strip runs to the screen's edges.
  • The seams: a seam sits above and below each section's content. A major seam opens a new movement of the page; a minor one continues a thought. seam-major runs 76 to 134px with the screen; seam-minor, two thirds of it, 51 to 89px. Two neighboring major sections stand 268px apart at full width and 152px on a phone.
  • No rule divides one section from the next. The page ends on a fixed 96px.
  • Spacing inside a section: sp-1 8px, sp-2 16px, sp-3 24 to 32px, sp-4 48 to 64px. head-gap, from a section's head to its content, is sp-4 less sp-3. Every three-up row sits on tri-cols with sp-3 between its columns.
  • Breakpoints: shared blocks draw theirs from one set: 480, 640, 720, 860, 900, 960, 1100, 1119, 1180, 1240, and 1360px.

The nav

  • The bar: sticky and frosted, black at 74% over a 20px blur. It is 75px tall: a 38px row with 18px above and below, over a 1px rule the screen's width.
  • Its three parts: the wordmark at the left, the links centered and 22px apart, one action at the right. The entry for the section on screen is lit. An anchor lands clear of the bar.
  • The links: 14px at the UI weight, in the second ink at rest and the first when lit, with a 1px underline at the foot of the link's box.
  • The action: the small button, drawn as a ghost: no fill, a hairline border. It reads "Get started" on both sites.
  • At the top of the page the blur is dropped: only black is under the bar. Where a browser has no backdrop blur, the bar is black at 92%.
  • 640px and under, khyzr.ai: the links are hidden, the row is 60px over its rule, and the frost is black at 88%. The bar's action waits while the hero's own is on screen.
  • 640px and under, khyzr.studio: the links drop to a second row and the bar is 103px. It folds to one row of 67px while the page goes down and returns on the way up. Without script it stays two rows.

  • Outfit only, at fw-display 600, fw-ui 500, and fw-text 400. Neither site loads Manrope, a serif, or a monospace.
  • The one exception is khyzr's Arabic name in the colophon: Noto Naskh Arabic at 1.1 times its line, as Type sets it.
  • Measures: the hero's headline 16ch, its lede 48ch, a continuation 34ch, a featured head 18ch.
  • The hero's headline steps down to between 34 and 54px at 1119px and under on both sites. At 720px and under it runs 34 to 44px on khyzr.ai and 34 to 42px on khyzr.studio.
  • Sentence case everywhere. Neither stylesheet holds an uppercase rule, an italic, or a positive tracking value. Tracking is negative on headings and none below them.
  • Headings balance their lines. Numbers that are compared take tabular, lining figures.
  • A button's label is 16px at the UI weight on a line height of 1, and 14px in the bar.

The section head

  • One centered column: a statement in fs-h2 in the first ink, over a continuation in fs-cont in the second ink.
  • The two share one weight and stand with no gap between them, so they read as two lines of one sentence.
  • No eyebrow, no number, no rule. head-gap separates the head from its content.

Focus and buttons

  • Focus: one ring on both sites, 2px with a 3px offset and a 4px radius. Its color is each site's own: a1 on khyzr.ai, ember-1 on khyzr.studio.
  • Buttons: one skeleton. A button is 44px tall with 18px sides on r-sm. The small one is 38px with 14px sides. The hero's is 40px with 22px sides on r-xs.
  • A press scales to .97. Nothing grows under the pointer.
  • Buttons are small-cornered rectangles, never pills. Their appearance is each site's own.

  • Square: lines (hairlines, ruled rows), bands that span the screen, and light or texture clipped by a rounded parent.
  • Nested: where a gap smaller than the outer radius separates two surfaces, the inner radius is the outer one less the gap, taken to the nearest step.
  • Off the ladder, by design: a radius that is a ratio of a drawn object. khyzr.ai's chat question; khyzr.studio's membership pass.

The closing and the colophon

Both sites end the same way. What fills the band is each site's own.

  • The closing: a plate the screen's width, square, one minor seam above the footer.
  • Its band: 140px across the plate's top, with a hairline beneath it. khyzr.ai fills it with its glyph marks; khyzr.studio with a dot grid lit from beneath.
  • The seal: the symbol, bare, on that hairline: 40px, or 32px at 720px and under. The hairline parts for it. It blooms once on arrival, pair by pair in the mark's own depth order.
  • The column: one, centered: a line in fs-feat, a lede, a 64px rule, one primary action.
  • The rim: one point of light in the site's ramp, lapping the plate's edge once in 14s.
  • The colophon: four columns in the proportion 2.4, 1, 1, 1.4: the wordmark and a line, then navigate, legal, and contact, which is the email alone. The labels are real headings.
  • The last line: the copyright with khyzr's Arabic name, then the build date, in the micro role. khyzr.ai's ends on its symbol as an end mark; khyzr.studio's carries none.

The scroll map, the wheel, and the entrance

  • The scroll map: from 1360px, with a fine pointer, a column 26px wide (map-w) at the screen's right edge takes the browser scrollbar's place. It holds one cluster of dots for each block of the page, moving at the page's pace under a marker at mid-screen. The page is laid out beside it.
  • The map is decoration; the nav is the navigation. Its inks are each site's own.
  • The wheel: a mouse wheel that steps is eased on a 110ms time constant. An in-page link glides for 1.4s at most. A trackpad, a touch, and the keys stay the browser's.
  • The entrance: a block wholly below the fold when the page starts arrives as it scrolls in, type rising by the line. Only position, scale, and opacity move.
  • Nothing eases in and nothing overshoots. Nothing is held under reduced motion or without script.

The marks on both sites

  • The wordmark is set inline, 20px tall, in the nav and at the top of the colophon. It links to the top of the page and is announced by the product's name. Its two fills are the site's own values.
  • The icon is never set beside the wordmark. The closing carries it bare as a seal, and the lost page carries it alone, between 56 and 72px.
  • The browser tab keeps the product's deep-tile badge in light and dark browsers alike.
  • One scheme. Both sites are dark only: a black theme color, and no light scheme anywhere.
  • khyzr on the page: in the product's name, and in the Arabic name on the copyright line. Neither site carries a khyzr logo, a line of endorsement, or a link to khyzr.com or to the other product.

What both sites leave out

As built, on both.

  • No label above a heading, and no label in capitals anywhere.
  • No numbered section and no "01" label.
  • No accent word in a headline, no gradient text, no italic.
  • No second typeface.
  • No arrow typed after a link's words.
  • No pill-shaped button.
  • No khyzr Green, and none of khyzr's backgrounds.
