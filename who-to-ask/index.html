Who to ask

Where a question about the brand goes, and what is approved before it is used.

One address.

Questions about the brand go to cisco@khyzr.com.

  • A file that is missing, a size that does not exist, a question these pages leave open: the same address.
  • New lockups are cut from the masters only, on request.
  • Partners, agencies, and printers ask there for the brand kit.

What is approved first.

One thing is always checked before it appears anywhere public: another company's mark.

  • It comes from that company's own brand or press page, as SVG or EPS.
  • It is approved before it is shown.
  • A mark on a slide is a claim. It appears only where the relationship is in writing.
  • The rules for showing it are on the Logos page.

Who approves what

The changeWho decides
Strategy, positioning, identity, creative directionThe brand owner
The brand's words: the tagline, boilerplate, product descriptors, the voice rulesThe brand owner
A design value, a mark, a pattern, a templateThe brand owner
A small call inside a locked system: a state, a spacing step, a minor layout choiceWhoever is doing the work, reported in one line
A release: the kit, the Drive, the public siteThe brand owner
A site's own page designThat site's repository, under its own rules
Showing another company's markThe brand owner, and only where the relationship is in writing
A new product's nameThe brand owner. It is khyzr.[x]
The registration, the legal lines, the company's accounts and domainsThe company

How to ask

  • You do not need to ask to use a template, a ready file, or anything the brand book shows.
  • Say what you are making, who it is for, and when it is due. Attach the file if one exists.
  • Ask before you publish, not after. If the brand book and the templates do not answer it, it is the brand owner's call.
  • For a client's or a partner's mark, send the official file from that company's own brand or press page, as SVG or EPS. It is checked, approved, and filed as a plate, so the next person does not repeat the work.
