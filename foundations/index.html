Radii

Four corners, and nothing in between.

  • radius-s — nav links and small chips.
  • radius-m — cards, the nav bar, code blocks.
  • radius-l — bands, grids, heroes.
  • radius-pill — every button. All buttons are pills.

Hairlines and shadows

Flat by default, depth on demand. Resting UI is bordered, not shadowed.

  • At rest, a surface carries a 1px hairline border and no shadow. Cards stay flat: white on white, separated by the hairline, with the alt band for rhythm.
  • Shadows appear only on hover and on floating elements — always soft, large-blur, negative-spread.
  • The nav in its paper state is shadowless: white at 97% over a hairline.
  • On dark surfaces, borders are white at 10–11% (on-dark-border) and dividers white at 10% (on-dark-divider). Dark surfaces take no shadows.

Motion

The Green Trace, in brief. The full grammar lives in the Motion chapter: springs move things, never colors; entrances overlap; nothing animates twice. Everything respects prefers-reduced-motion, which shows the final states.

Reveal — the root-anchored rise

Things grow in. An entrance rises rise-distance to rest and scales from rise-scale to 1, anchored at the element's baseline, over dur-enter on the ease-trace spring. Items in a group follow dur-stagger apart, overlapping.
