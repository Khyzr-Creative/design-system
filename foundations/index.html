Design system · Foundations

Foundations

The structure every khyzr page stands on. One column and its measures, four corners, hairlines and the few shadows, keyboard focus, three layers, and a spacing scale of six steps.

Layout

One column, generous air. The column is container wide at most, centered, inside gutter margins. Reading text never runs the column's width.

  • A page stands on paper. surface-2 sets a section apart from its white neighbors.
  • A page ends in the dark: pitch and ink for its last sections, and a footer that is always ink. green-900 carries a dark element inside a light section.
  • A section pads its sides by gutter, or by a phone's safe inset where that is wider.
  • section-pad-y stands between a section's content and any change of ground, the same on both sides of the edge. Two neighbors on one ground share the seam.
  • A lede runs to measure-lede at most. measure-reading is the widest a single large reading line runs.

Breakpoint

  • One width is shared by all three sites: break-phone. At and below it a layout takes its phone form.
  • Every other change belongs to the section that needs it. A section restacks where its own content asks, and that width is not a token.
  • A media query cannot read a custom property, so the query types the value and the token names it.

Radii

Four corners, and nothing in between.

  • radius-s: small chips, and the focus shape of a text link.
  • radius-m: cards, icon tiles, form plates, code blocks.
  • radius-l: bands, grids, heroes, a photograph.
  • radius-pill: every button, a navigation link, a round control. All buttons are pills.
  • The four are for surfaces and controls. A mark smaller than a chip stands outside them: a dot is a full circle, and a thin rule or a chart's bar rounds its own ends.

Hairlines and shadows

Flat by default. A shadow appears only where something leaves the page.

  • At rest a surface carries a 1px hairline and no shadow, or a surface-2 fill on white.
  • Under the pointer a control's border darkens to hairline-strong.
  • shadow-lift falls under a card that lifts on hover. shadow-button falls under the primary pill on hover.
  • shadow-float belongs to the one kind of thing that floats at rest, a sheet on a pile. shadow-held is the same sheet while it is held.
  • Shadows are soft and large, pulled in under the element by a negative spread. They are tinted with the deep greens. Only the button's is black, under an ink pill.
  • On dark, a border is on-dark-border and a divider on-dark-divider. A dark surface casts no shadow.
  • Type over a moving picture may sit on a soft shade of the ground's own color, so it stays readable. That is a shade behind type, not a shadow under a surface.
  • The one light on dark is the green dot's glow, in green-300 and green-400.

Keyboard focus

Every control shows where the keyboard is.

Book a conversation

focus-ring, focus-width wide, focus-offset outside

Book a conversation

focus-ring-on-dark, the same width and offset

  • An outline focus-width wide in focus-ring, focus-offset outside the element. On a dark ground the outline is focus-ring-on-dark.
  • The outline follows the element's own corners. A text link's focus shape takes radius-s.
  • It shows on keyboard focus only, and it is never removed without a replacement.
  • Inside a product's own page, the ring takes that product's accent.

Selection

Selected text is tinted with selection, a translucent khyzr Green.

Layers

Three things stand above a page, in this order.

TokenValueUse
z-nav60the fixed navigation
z-scrollbar70a custom scrollbar, over the navigation
z-skip100the skip link, over everything
  • z-nav is the fixed navigation. z-scrollbar is a custom scrollbar, over it. z-skip is the skip link, over everything.
  • Inside a section, stacking stays local: values from 0 to 3, in a section that isolates its own stack. Nothing inside a section reaches the three layers above.

Six steps.

  • Every new distance comes from the scale.
  • khyzr.com states the same values as their rem twins, so they follow its desktop scale.
  • A distance inside one component may sit between two steps. Those belong to the component, and no token holds them.

Motion

Things grow in. The curves, the clocks, and the rules for everything that moves are on the Motion page. Two of them shape a layout:

  • A block arrives once, rising rise-distance from its own baseline. Leave it the room to rise.
  • Everything respects reduced motion, which shows final states. A page reads whole without any of it.
