Design system · Interface

Forms

Plate fields. A field is a filled plate with no border at rest, and turns white under a green ring on focus. Soft, roomy, and quiet until you touch it.

The field

Quiet at rest, white when awake. The plate is the affordance, so a resting field needs no border. Green appears only on focus and on success: the rare-accent rule holds here too.

The plate field: a filled plate with no border at rest, white with a green ring on focus. The label is always visible, above the field.

<PlateField label="Work email" placeholder="you@company.com" helper="One line of help sits here." />
<PlateField label="What do you need?" textarea rows={5} />

States: rest (a surface-2 plate, no border) · focus (white, a khyzr-green border, a soft 3px ring) · error (the message takes the helper's place, over a 6% wash) · disabled (the whole group at 45%). Set onDark on ink and green-900.

  • Field text is Outfit at 15px, on a line-height of 1.45.
  • The label is 13px at fw-ui, always visible, above the field. No floating label, and no placeholder standing in for one.
  • Helper text sits under the field: 12.5px in muted, one line.
  • A field changes color on ease-standard over dur-color. Nothing in a form springs.
  • The field's own ring is its focus mark, so it takes no second outline.

Controls

The same plate, in every shape. The field and the textarea are built, as the PlateField primitive. The other three are specified here and not yet built.

ControlThe ruleState
TextareaThe plate, resizing vertically. Four rows by defaultBuilt
SelectThe plate with a chevron at the right: a line 1.5 thick in muted. It focuses like the fieldSpecified
Choice pillsFor a single pick. Plate-filled pills at radius-pill, each with a native radio inside. An unselected pill carries muted text and a border that shows on hover. The selected pill takes the khyzr-green border and ink textSpecified

Messages

An error speaks, and a success nods.

  • Error. The message takes the helper's place: the same slot, never both. It sets at 12.5px in error.
  • An error says what to do, plainly and without blame: "Enter an email address."
  • Success. A quiet green line beside the action, with the check. Not a modal, and not a banner.
  • The action is always the pill, and a form has one primary action.

On dark

The same geometry, set for ink and green-900. On the primitive, set onDark.

  • Green-bright takes over for focus and success, as the accent does everywhere on dark.
  • #E08A80 is the error's color lifted for a dark ground. It is the field's own, and no token holds it.

Rules

DoDon't
Keep the label visible, above the fieldA floating label, or a placeholder as the label
One primary action to a form: the pillGreen on anything but focus and success
Let the error take the helper's slotA border on a resting field
Ask only what the conversation needsA toast, a modal, or a red banner for validation

In use

No khyzr site carries a form yet: each ends in a pill that opens a conversation. The field is ready for the first surface that needs one.
