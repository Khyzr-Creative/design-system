Forms

Plate fields — filled plates with no border at rest, white with a green ring on focus. Soft, roomy and quiet until you touch them.

The field

Quiet at rest, white when awake. The plate is the affordance, so a resting field needs no border. Green appears only on focus and success — the rare-accent rule holds here too. The Plate direction was adopted on 2026-07-13.

khyzr "Plate" form field — filled plate at rest, white with a green ring on focus; label always visible.

<PlateField label="Work email" placeholder="you@company.com" helper="We reply within two working days." />
<PlateField label="What do you need?" textarea rows={5} />

States: rest (plate #F5F7F3, no border) · focus (white + green border + 3px soft ring) · error (message replaces helper, 6% wash) · disabled (group 45%). Set onDark on ink/green-900 surfaces.

  • Rest — a surface-2 plate, transparent border, radius 14, padding 13 × 16.
  • Focus — the plate turns white and takes a khyzr-green border with a soft 3px ring of green at 14%.
  • Filled — the value sits on the resting plate.
  • Disabled — the whole group at 45% opacity, label included.
  • Field text is Outfit at 15px.
  • Labels are 13px at 600, always visible, above the field — no floating labels, no placeholder-as-label.
  • Helper text sits under the field: 12.5px, faint, one line.

Controls

The same plate, every shape.

  • Select — the plate with a chevron at the right, a 1.5-stroke line in muted. It focuses like the field.
  • Textarea — the plate, resizing vertically.
  • Choice pills — for a single pick. Plate-filled pills at radius-pill, each with a native radio inside. Unselected pills carry muted text and a border that shows on hover; the selected pill earns the green border and ink text.
  • Radios and checkboxes — native controls, tinted with accent-color set to khyzr Green.

Semantic states

Error speaks, success nods.

  • Error — the border turns error over a 6% wash, and the message replaces the helper line: same slot, never both. The message sets at 12.5px, 600, in error.
  • Write error messages in voice: plain, direct, no blame.
  • Success — a quiet green line beside the action, with a check. Not a modal, not a banner.
  • Submit is always the existing pill, and each form has one primary action.

On ink

The dark-band companion: the same geometry, translated for green-900 — where the CTA band's booking form lives. On the primitive, set onDark.

  • Plate — white at 8%.
  • Focus — white at 12%, a green-bright border, a ring at 18%.
  • Error — #E08A80 over a 10% wash.
  • Labels set in on-dark-soft.
  • Green-bright takes over for focus and success, matching the on-dark accent everywhere else.
  • Submit flips to the white pill, under a white rule at 14%.
