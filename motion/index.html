Design system · Foundations

Motion

The Green Trace. Things grow in. Four curves, seven clocks, and the rules for everything that moves on a khyzr page.

The Green Trace

khyzr's motion behaves like growth. An element rises crisp from its own baseline, with a breath of overshoot, and overlaps the one before it as it enters. Never a queue, never a flicker, never out of focus.

It serves a line from the voice:

"They say grass grew wherever he stood. We hold ourselves to that: where we work, things should be growing that weren't before."

Grammar

Four rules, no exceptions.

  • Springs move things, never colors. The overshoot curves apply to transforms only. Color, background, border, and opacity change on ease-standard. A hue cannot bounce.
  • Entrances overlap. Grouped elements start one dur-stagger apart, while the one before is still moving. Growth, not a queue.
  • Nothing animates twice. One arrival for each element in a page view. Coming back to a section does not replay it. Ambient loops are the only repeaters: a hero's orb, a marquee, a turning wall of quotes, the mesh's drift.
  • Motion is a privilege. Reduced motion gets final states at once: no autoplay, and hovers keep working without transforms. No GPU means a still, never a broken simulation.

Motion is a behavior of screens. Print, decks, and collateral do not animate.

Tokens

The curves, plotted, and the clocks.

  • dur-enter 900ms entrances
  • dur-stagger 140ms the step between entrances, overlapping
  • dur-hover 500ms hovers
  • dur-pulse 420ms the settle pulse
  • dur-count 1600ms count-ups
  • dur-color 200ms a color, a border or a ground answering the pointer, on the standard ease
  • dur-surface 350ms a surface changing state, on the standard ease: the navigation's flip
  • rise-distance 30px how far an entrance rises
  • rise-scale .96 the scale an entrance grows from

Curves

CurveUse
ease-traceentrances and big moves: a soft spring with a slight overshoot
ease-pophovers and pulses: a livelier overshoot for small distances
ease-driftgrowing bars, widths, and rules, and a card's lift: decisive, with no overshoot, since a bar cannot shrink past zero
ease-standardcolor, background, border, and opacity

Clocks

Distances

  • Rise: rise-distance.
  • Entrance scale: from rise-scale to 1, anchored at the baseline, the element's bottom center.
  • Card lift: 4px.
  • Button grow: 1.05. Under a press: .98, and .95 for a round control.
  • Arrow slide: 4px.
  • Settle pulse: 1.07.

The standard ease

What does not move, eases.

  • A change of color, background, border, or opacity runs on ease-standard, between 200 and 400ms.
  • dur-color is the quick end: a link, a border, or a plate under the pointer.
  • dur-surface is the slow end: a whole surface changing state.
  • A rising block's opacity fades in beside its rise, over 800 to 900ms. A photograph develops in over 700ms once it has loaded.

Primitives

The moves a page is built from.

Section entrance

A block rises rise-distance and scales up from rise-scale, grown from its baseline, on ease-trace over dur-enter. Crisp the whole way: no blur, no filter.

A title rises
Then its lede
Then the rest

Overlapping stagger

When several elements enter together, each starts one dur-stagger after the last, in reading order, overlapping the one before it. Like items in a set may follow closer.

Count-up and settle pulse

A number counts up from zero over dur-count, easing out, then lands with a pulse to 1.07 on ease-pop over dur-pulse. Under reduced motion the final value is already in the markup.

24A figure counts up for dur-count, then settles with a dur-pulse.

Hovers and presses

A button grows to 1.05 and an arrow slides 4px, on ease-pop over dur-hover. A card comes up to the paper: it lifts 4px on ease-drift, turns from surface-2 to white, and takes shadow-lift. Under a press a pill settles to .98.

CardLifts 4px on hover
A color changes on the standard ease
  • A hover that moves or reveals something applies only where the pointer can hover. A press answers every pointer.
  • An outlined pill on dark stays color-only: its border brightens, and nothing grows.

Charts

Charts obey the Green Trace. Bars grow from their baseline on ease-drift. Numbers count up over dur-count with the settle pulse. One arrival for each chart in a page view: nothing loops, and nothing animates twice.
