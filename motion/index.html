09 · Expression

Motion

The Green Trace — things grow in. The curves, clocks and rules for everything that moves.

The Green Trace

khyzr's motion behaves like growth. Elements rise crisp from their own baseline — scale anchored at the ground line, a breath of overshoot — and overlap as they enter. Never a queue, never a flicker, never out of focus. Adopted on 2026-07-14.

It serves a line from the voice:

"When we leave, things should be growing that weren't before."

Grammar

Four rules, no exceptions.

  • Springs move things, never colors. Overshoot curves apply to transforms only. Color, background and opacity change on plain ease — a hue can't bounce.
  • Entrances overlap. Grouped elements start one dur-stagger apart, while the previous one is still moving — growth, not a queue.
  • Nothing animates twice. One arrival per element per page view. Re-entering a section does not replay it. Ambient loops (marquee, quotes) are the only repeaters.
  • Motion is a privilege. Reduced motion gets final states instantly — no autoplay, and hovers keep working without transforms. No GPU means static fallbacks, never a broken sim.

Tokens

Curves and clocks.

  • dur-enter 900ms
  • dur-stagger 140ms overlapping
  • dur-hover 500ms
  • dur-pulse 420ms
  • dur-count 1600ms
  • rise-distance 30px
  • rise-scale .96

Curves

CurveUse
ease-traceentrances and big moves — a soft spring, slight overshoot
ease-pophovers and pulses — a livelier overshoot for small distances
ease-driftgrowing bars and widths — decisive, no overshoot (a bar can't shrink past zero)
plain ease, 200–350mscolor, background, border, opacity — including the nav paper-flip

Clocks

ClockUse
dur-enterentrances
dur-staggerthe stagger step, overlapping
dur-hoverhovers
dur-pulsethe settle pulse
dur-countcount-ups

Distances

  • Rise: rise-distance.
  • Entrance scale: from rise-scale to 1, anchored at the baseline (the element's bottom center).
  • Card lift: −3px, with a −.4° tilt and scale 1.015.
  • Button grow: 1.05.
  • Settle pulse: 1.07.

Primitives

The four moves.

Section entrance

Entrances run on ease-trace over dur-enter: a rise of rise-distance and a scale up from rise-scale, grown from the baseline. Crisp the whole way — no blur, no filter.

Overlapping stagger

When several elements enter together, each starts one dur-stagger after the last, overlapping the one before it.

Count-up and settle pulse

Numbers count up over dur-count, then land with a settle pulse to 1.07 on ease-pop over dur-pulse.

24A figure counts up for dur-count, then settles with a dur-pulse.

Hovers

Hovers run on ease-pop over dur-hover. Buttons grow to 1.05. Cards grow toward you: they lift −3px, tilt −.4° and scale to 1.015.
