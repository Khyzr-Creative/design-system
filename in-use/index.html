Brand · In use

In use

The brand at work. khyzr's own pieces, each shown as a finished object, with where to find its template.

Presentations.

The khyzr deck is white. Titles are sentences in ink, with one green phrase at most, and the wordmark holds the bottom left of every working slide.

  • It opens on the mesh, set as a panel on the white slide. It closes on Ink, signed with the tagline.
  • One idea to a slide. The title says it, and the slide proves it.
  • Twenty layouts, for words, pictures, numbers, charts, and people.
  • Template: khyzr presentation, for PowerPoint, Google Slides, and Keynote. In the Drive's Templates folder, under Presentations, and in the brand kit.
  • khyzr.ai and khyzr.studio each have a deck of their own, in their own folders.

Documents.

A khyzr document is a white page with the wordmark at its top left and one calm column of Outfit.

  • Five to start from: the letter, the memo, the proposal, the report, the one-pager.
  • Green is small on the page: one phrase in a title, a list's numerals, the dot in the folio.
  • Template: one for each, for Word and Google Docs. In Templates, under Documents.
  • How a document is built is on the Documents page.

The letter.

The letterhead is the quietest piece khyzr makes: the wordmark, one line at the foot, and white paper.

  • The wordmark sits at the top left, with its clear space around it.
  • No background and no pattern. A letter is white.
  • Template: the khyzr letter, for Word and Google Docs. In Templates, under Documents. The print-ready letterhead is under Print.

The business card.

A card is small, so it carries little: a name, a role, the ways to reach them, and the wordmark.

  • The front is white, with the name in ink and the wordmark.
  • The back is the one small place for the brand at full voice: the deep green, flat, with the wordmark at its center.
  • Two sizes: 85 by 55 mm, and 3.5 by 2 in.
  • Template: a print-ready file, with an editable source. In Templates, under Print.

The email signature.

A signature is text first. It must read in any mail program, with pictures on or off.

  • The name, the role, one phone number, khyzr.com, and the wordmark. No email address.
  • Set in Arial, since an email cannot carry Outfit. See Type.
  • No tagline, no banner, no second logo.
  • Template: in Templates, under Email signature.

LinkedIn.

The company page, each person's profile, and the posts.

  • The avatar is the badge, filling the square.
  • The cover is the mesh. The company page and a personal profile each have their own shape.
  • A post is white first. The mesh is for an opener, and the logo pattern for a rare announcement.
  • The share card is the brand's card for links: the dark mesh with the lockup at its center.
  • Template: in Templates, under LinkedIn.

Meeting backgrounds.

The mesh, behind the person, in either look.

  • Dark or light: the one your own picture reads best against.
  • The logo pattern is kept for an event khyzr hosts.
  • Files: in Templates, under Meeting backgrounds.

The three sites.

Each site is its brand at its most complete. The sites are the reference for the look.

  • khyzr.com opens on white, with ink type and green as the rare accent. It ends dark.
  • khyzr.ai stands on black, with its ramp from periwinkle to aqua.
  • khyzr.studio stands on black, with its ember ramp from yellow to rose.
  • None of the three uses a background pattern. See Backgrounds.
  • How each is built is in the design system: khyzr.com, khyzr.ai, khyzr.studio.
