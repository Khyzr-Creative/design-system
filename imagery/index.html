Imagery

Honest documentary. Real people mid-work, real rooms, natural light — photography as evidence that we're actually there.

The look

Candid, calm, uncorrected. Photos are the human register; the green stays in UI, pattern and type. The direction was adopted on 2026-07-13.

Photo: Pexels

  • Grade: saturate(.88) contrast(1.02). That's the whole grade, fixed for every photo.
  • No green cast, no HDR, no heavy vignettes, no film grain. Photos are never green-tinted.
  • If a photo needs more correction than this to look right, it's the wrong photo.
  • Frame: media radius radius-m. A photo sits on paper or ink surfaces, never on a pattern surface — one texture per band. Photo and pattern never share a band.
  • Two photo surfaces per viewport at most.

Illustration

We don't draw — with two sanctioned exceptions. Photography, the pattern and the icon set carry all meaning. No new drawing practice exists: no character mascots, isometric scenes, 3D blobs, hand-drawn doodles or AI spot art — anywhere. The policy was adopted on 2026-07-16.

The floor is the rule. The two extensions below are the only exceptions, and both reuse languages the system already owns.

Pattern as spot art

  • Seeded crops of <khyzr-pattern> only — never redrawn shapes.
  • One spot per surface, its seed fixed and logged.
  • Scope: empty states, 404, email headers, deck section breaks.
  • The pattern caps hold: two pattern surfaces per viewport at most, never behind long copy.
