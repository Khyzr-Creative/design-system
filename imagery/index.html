Brand · The look

Imagery

Honest documentary. Real people mid-work, real rooms, natural light. Photography is the evidence that we are actually there.

Real people, mid-work.

Candid, calm, uncorrected. Photographs are the human register of the brand. The green stays in the type, the backgrounds, and the marks.

  • People are mid-work: not posing, and not looking at the camera.
  • The light belongs to the room. No studio backdrops, no colored gels, no lens flare.
  • One moment, calmly framed. If it looks like an advert, it is the wrong photo.

What we shoot, and what we never shoot.

The test is whether it could be a real Tuesday.

DoDon't
Working sessions mid-conversation: whiteboards, marked-up plans, a shared laptopPosed handshakes, suits at glass towers, skyline panoramas
Hands doing the work, and rooms as they actually areTinting a photo green, or turning it to two tones
Natural or available light, subjects off-center, room to breatheStaged stock scenes of any kind
Client teams alongside ours, with dignityPhotography as wallpaper

One light grade.

Every photograph takes the same small correction, and nothing else.

  • Saturation down 12%. Contrast up 2%. That is the whole grade.
  • No green cast, no heavy vignette, no film grain, no high-dynamic-range look.
  • If a photo needs more than this to look right, it is the wrong photo.

How a photograph is framed.

A photograph is a window: rounded at the corners, or run to the edges.

  • Rounded corners, or full bleed. Nothing in between, and never a circle.
  • It sits on white or Ink. Never on a background pattern: one texture to a surface.
  • Text over a photograph needs a solid field behind it: a panel or a dark scrim.
  • Two photographs in view at most.

Where photographs come from.

Stock stands in today. An owned shoot replaces it.

  • Now. Free, candid stock, passed through the do and don't above.
  • Search for the activity and the place, not the concept. Take the largest size on offer.
  • The starter set. Twenty photographs, chosen against this page, in the Drive's photos folder. A find worth using again joins the set.
  • The owned shoot. One documentary half-day: a working session at the board, two people over one laptop, hands on a printed plan, the room empty with the lights on.

We don't draw.

Photography, the backgrounds, and the icons carry the meaning. khyzr has no illustration style, on purpose.

  • No character mascots, no isometric scenes, no 3D blobs, no hand-drawn doodles, no AI spot art.
  • One exception: a crop of the mesh. As a spot of texture on an empty state, an email header, or a section break. One to a surface, cropped from a ready image, never redrawn.
  • One exception: a line diagram that explains. A flow, a system, a timeline, in a deck or a document. Thin lines, kin to the icons: Forest on light, light green on dark. Labels in the label face, and one terracotta flag for the thing to see. Never decoration.

Generated images.

An image made by an AI tool has two uses, and no others.

  • A mockup scene. A deck on a laptop, a card on a desk: a setting that shows a khyzr piece in the world.
  • A texture. A paper, a surface, a material.
  • Never people. The direction rests on the people being real.
  • Never anything passed off as a documentary photograph.
