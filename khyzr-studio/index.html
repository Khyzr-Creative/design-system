khyzr.studio

Design on subscription for business clients. khyzr.studio keeps khyzr's type and frame and brings its own colour — a warm near-black ground and a five-step ember ramp, with the .studio yellow as the brand colour.

Shared and its own

The two product sites are one system with two colours. What they hold in common is a vocabulary, not a design.

  • From khyzr: the mark system. The product wordmark is the corporate wordmark with .studio; the product icon is the corporate symbol in this product's ramp. The corporate rules in Logos still govern the corporate mark.
  • With khyzr.ai: the frame, the type scale, the nav, the closing, the colophon, focus, the button skeletons and the radius ladder, shared by name and structure.
  • Its own: colour, the hero, the engines, and every section's content.
  • Type: Outfit only, at three weights by role — khyzr's one family, without the technical face: the product sites load no Manrope. See Type.

The mark

Product marks follow one set of rules — icon or wordmark, never both; the icon's structure; the tiles and favicons — kept in Logos. This page holds only what is this product's own.

  • On dark: khyzr in the site's off-white, .studio in ember-1 #F7CD3A.
  • On light: khyzr in the site's ground #0E0C0A; .studio turns ember-3 #F38239. So the .studio accent changes with the ground: ember-1 on dark, ember-3 on white.
  • One color: the product wordmark in the corporate mono values, black and white.

Ember ramp

  • Structure: a warm ground ladder, two levels and a hairline, with bone and ash for ink, and the ember ramp — an even staircase from yellow to rose, each step falling the same in lightness and in hue.
  • The brand colour is ember-1, the .studio yellow. Every flat accent reads it: the wordmark's .studio, selection, link and nav underlines, hover borders, the offer's ticks.
  • ember-3 is the hot stop. The ramp itself carries the gradients, the card and the bands, and the five pillars take its steps in order.
  • Ember is a state. At rest the page is ash, bone and hairlines; ember arrives on response, arrival and focus.
  • One page-scale colour commit: the membership band.
  • Texture: the dot grid lives in the rails only; grain is the one full-viewport texture.
  • Contrast: text holds 4.5:1 or more; ember is a state, not text. Hairlines sit at 1.23:1 by choice.
