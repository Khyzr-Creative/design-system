What shifts in print.

Process ink cannot reach khyzr's deep greens. They print a little duller and yellower than the screen shows.

  • It affects khyzr Green, Forest, Deep Forest, the deep green, and the marks' own greens.
  • Everything else in the palette prints as it looks.
  • Where the green must be exact, a cover, a sign, a card, ask for a spot ink.
  • No Pantone number is set for any khyzr color. The printer matches a spot ink by eye, against a printed swatch book.
  • Give the printer the screen value and the coated value, and approve the match on paper, not on a screen.
  • Ask for a printed proof before a first run.

For screens only.

Some values exist only for interfaces, and have no life off the screen.

  • The ten steps of the green scale, the grays of text on dark grounds, and the colors of hover, focus, and selection.
  • They are in the design system, on Tokens.
  • Off the screen, use the named colors on this page.

The swatch file.

khyzr-palette.ase, for Illustrator, InDesign, and Photoshop. It holds the eighteen colors of the first table, twice.

  • One group for screens, in RGB. One group for print, in process ink for coated paper.
  • Each swatch is named in plain words. A print swatch's name ends ", print".
  • khyzr.ai and khyzr.studio each have a swatch file of their own, with their product's files.
  • It is in the Drive's Fonts and color folder, and in the brand kit.
