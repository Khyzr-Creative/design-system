10 · Interface

Components

Every pattern on the site, recreated one to one — actions, navigation, the section header, cards and bands, accents, proof and voices, and the footer. The rules sit with each pattern.

Buttons and links

Every button is a pill, radius-pill — never a square button. The primary CTA reads "Book a conversation", always; never "Get started", "Learn more" or "Contact us".

khyzr pill button — every button in the system is a 999-radius pill; primary CTA copy is always "Book a conversation".

<Button variant="primary" size="l" href="#contact">Book a conversation</Button>

Variants: primary (ink, default) · white (on green-900/ink bands) · ghost (outlined, dark surfaces only — never on light). Hover grows 1.05 on the pop spring. Never invent square buttons.

PropTypeNotes

size'm' | 'l'
hrefstringRenders an <a> when set.
onClick() => void
disabledboolean
children requiredReact.ReactNode
  • Primary — the ink pill with white text. It is the default.
  • Secondary, on light — a transparent pill with a hairline border. On hover the border darkens and the fill turns white.
  • Text link — ink text with an arrow that slides 4px on hover.
  • On dark bands — the white pill leads on green-900 and ink. The hero ghost is white at 8% with a light blur and a white border at 36%; the footer ghost is a white border at 25% that brightens to 60% on hover.
  • Ghost — the outlined pill lives on dark surfaces only, never on light.
  • Hover — buttons grow 1.05 on ease-pop over dur-hover, and arrows slide on the same curve. Ghost buttons stay color-only.
  • Focus — a 2px focus-ring outline at a 3px offset; white on dark.
  • A quiet reassurance aside can sit under a CTA — one of the voice's signature moves.

Navigation

The paper-flip bar: full width, fixed, 64px tall, set on the container's gutters. It has two states and one change between them.

Over the hero

  • Transparent, with no border.
  • Links are white at 78%, going full white on a faint white plate on hover.
  • The CTA is the white pill.

Past the hero

  • Once the page scrolls past 72% of the hero's height, the bar flips to paper at 97% with a hairline below. It stays shadowless.
  • Links are muted, going ink on a surface-2 plate on hover.
  • The CTA flips to the ink pill.

Both states

  • The flip is a color and surface change on standard ease (350ms) — never a spring.
  • The brand is the symbol, 30px high, above its 24px floor, crossfading from on-dark to on-light. The wordmark stays in the footer.
  • The CTA reads "Book a conversation" in both states.
  • The glass and green-900 bars are retired.

Section header

Eyebrow, then title, then lede — the way every section opens.

Numbered technical-label section eyebrow (Manrope, uppercase, tracked) with the green dash — starts every khyzr section.

<Eyebrow num="01">What we do</Eyebrow>
Props
PropTypeNotes
numstringChapter/section number, e.g. "01".
onDarkboolean
children requiredReact.ReactNode
  • The eyebrow is numbered, in the technical register, behind the green dash: "01 · What we do". On dark it turns green-bright.
  • The title sets at text-title, tracked tight at tracking-display. One green word per title, at most — the payoff word.
  • The lede sets at text-lede in muted, held to measure-lede.
  • The header block runs to 760px wide, with 44–72px of fluid space below it.

Cards and bands

The containers.

Discipline grid — hairline cells

  • Cells sit in a grid whose 1px gaps are painted by the hairline behind them. The outer corner is radius-l; cells fill minmax(290px, 1fr).
  • A cell rests on surface-2 and lifts to white on hover, while a 20px green tick slides out beside its title.
  • Each cell carries a numeral, 01 to 06, in the technical register, then a title and one line in muted.

Partner card — lifts on hover

  • White surface, a hairline border, radius-m, 30px padding.
  • A green lane label in the technical register sits above the name and a one-line description.
  • On hover the card lifts −3px, tilts −.4° and scales 1.015 on ease-pop, and takes the soft hover shadow.
  • Cards run in a grid of minmax(240px, 1fr) with an 18px gap.

Statement band — ink and a green bloom

  • An ink ground at radius-l, with a soft radial green bloom in the upper right.
  • Text in on-dark-soft, the bold phrase in white, one highlight in green-bright, on-dark-accent.

Principle item — ruled top

  • Each principle sits under a 2px hairline rule: a title, then one line in muted.
  • The lead variant takes a green rule — it marks the anchor principle.
  • Principles run in a grid of minmax(180px, 1fr), gap 36 / 30.

Accents

A small vocabulary.

  • Pillar chips — a word at display weight, led by a 7px green dot.
  • Scroll cue — a 1×32px bar fading downward, pulsing on a 2.4s cycle, above a technical label.
  • Accent phrase — the payoff words of a line in green.

Status chips

The Badge primitive is the system's chip grammar for status — not the logo badge. Solid is decided or locked, outline is status, neutral is quiet meta, mint is an ID chip.

Status pill in the technical face (Manrope) — the system's badge grammar.

<Badge tone="solid">locked</Badge> <Badge>adopted 07-16</Badge>

Mint tone uses deep forest text on pale mint (8.9:1) — never green-600 on mint at small sizes.

Proof and voices

The proof band and client quotes.

Proof stats — the count-up pair

Display-scale proof number — deep forest numeral, green unit mark, technical-label caption (Manrope).

<ProofStat value="25" suffix="+" label="companies" />
Props
PropTypeNotes

  • A light band with a hairline below. A pair of display-scale numerals, each with its unit mark in green and a caption in the technical register, set off from the logo row by a hairline divider.
  • Numbers count up from zero on first reveal over dur-count and land with a settle pulse of 1.07. Under reduced motion they show their final values.
  • Client logos roll beside them in a 30-second marquee, dark on transparent on the light band.

Voices — the rotating quote band

  • Green-900 at radius-l, with the dark <khyzr-pattern> on one fixed seed, centered.
  • The quote sets in Outfit at display weight, held to about 44 characters a line. Beneath it, the client's logo in white on transparent, the name in white and the role in a muted on-dark grey.
  • Quotes advance every 8 seconds and pause on hover. The current dot is green-bright; the others are white at 24%.

Footer

The dark closing. The footer is always Ink.

  • on-dark-borderrgba(255,255,255,.11)
  • Top row — a headline held to 14ch with one green-bright accent word, and the footer ghost pill, "Book a conversation", with its arrow. An on-dark-divider rule runs below.
  • Columns — the brand column at 1.4fr holds the on-dark wordmark and one line; the link columns at 1fr each are headed in the technical register in on-dark-faint.
  • Links — on-dark-body, turning white on hover. External links sit quieter, in on-dark-muted, with the outward arrow.
  • Base row — a green dot before the copyright line; legal links in on-dark-faint.
  • The on-ink greys are the on-dark tokens — on-dark-body, on-dark-muted and on-dark-faint — not one-off values.
