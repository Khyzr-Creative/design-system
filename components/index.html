Design system · Interface

Components

The primitives the system builds, and the rules each one carries: the button, the eyebrow, the status chip, the proof figure, and the plate field. A primitive reads the tokens and nothing else. khyzr.com's own navigation, sections, and footer are on its as-built page.

What is built

Five primitives. Each is one React function, with its props typed beside it and a short guide.

PrimitiveWhat it isWhere it is shown
ButtonThe pill, in four looks and two sizesButtons and links, below
EyebrowThe numbered label that opens a section in a documentSection header, below
BadgeThe status chipStatus chips, below
ProofStatThe proof figureProof, below
PlateFieldThe form fieldThe Forms page
  • A primitive takes its look from the tokens, so it follows the token files and carries no stylesheet of its own.
  • This site serves all five as one script, components.js. It sets them on window.KhyzrComponents and needs React 18 on the page.
  • Anything larger than a primitive belongs to the page that builds it. khyzr.com's nav, cards, sheets, and footer are recorded on its own page, as patterns to draw from.

Buttons and links

Every button is a pill, at radius-pill. khyzr's primary action reads "Book a conversation"; a product's reads "Get started". Never "Learn more", "Contact us", or "Book a demo".

The khyzr button. Every button in the system is a pill. The primary action always reads "Book a conversation".

<Button variant="primary" size="l" href="#contact">Book a conversation</Button>
<Button variant="secondary" size="l" href="#work">Explore the work</Button>

Variants: primary (the ink pill, the default) · secondary (a hairline pill, on light) · white (on dark) · ghost (outlined, on dark only, for a second action beside the white pill). Sizes are khyzr.com's two pills: m, the compact one in the nav (13.5px type, 40px tall), and l, the page's (15px type, 14px by 26px of padding). On hover a pill grows to 1.05 on the pop spring; the page's primary pill also deepens to black and takes shadow-button, the secondary turns white under hairline-strong, and the ghost only brightens its border. A press settles to .98. Keyboard focus draws the system's ring outside the pill, white on dark. Never a square button.

Props
PropTypeNotes

size'm' | 'l'm = the compact pill, 40px tall, as in the nav (default); l = the page's pill, as in the hero.

The four looks

LookWhat it isGround
PrimaryThe ink pill with white text. The defaultLight
SecondaryA transparent pill with a hairline border and ink textLight
WhiteThe white pill with ink textDark
GhostAn outlined pill with white text, for a second action beside the white pillDark only
  • One primary action stands in a view. A second action beside it takes the secondary on light, or the ghost on dark.
  • The pill comes in two sizes. The compact pill sets 13.5px type in a pill 40px tall, as in a nav. The page's pill sets 15px type with 14px by 26px of padding, as in a hero.
  • On a touch screen a pill is never under 44px tall.
  • A quiet line of reassurance can sit under a primary action.

States

StateWhat changes
HoverThe pill grows to 1.05 on ease-pop over dur-hover. The page's primary pill also deepens to black and takes shadow-button. The secondary turns white under a hairline-strong border. The ghost only brightens its border
PressThe pill settles to .98
FocusAn outline focus-width wide in focus-ring, focus-offset away from the pill. On dark the outline is focus-ring-on-dark
DisabledThe pill dims to 45% and leaves the pointer's reach
  • A color changes on ease-standard over dur-color. Only a transform takes a spring.
  • A hover that moves something applies only where the pointer can hover. A press answers every pointer.
  • The focus outline shows for the keyboard, and never for a click.

Links

  • Text link. Ink text at fw-ui with the right arrow after it. The arrow slides 4px on hover, on ease-pop. Its hit area is 44px tall.
  • A link in running text. The text's own color over a green-200 underline, which turns khyzr-green on hover.
  • A link in a document, or on a reference page. link, which is khyzr-green, with no underline.
  • A link on dark. on-dark-body, turning white on hover over a green-300 underline. A link that leaves the site is quieter, in on-dark-muted, with the outward arrow.

Round controls

  • A circle with a hairline border and one icon from the set, for a control that has no words: pause and play, previous and next, close.
  • 44px across at the least. The border darkens to hairline-strong on hover, and the control settles to .95 under a press.
  • Every round control carries a label for a screen reader, since it shows no words.

Section header

A section opens on its title, then its lede.

The eyebrow: a numbered label in the technical register (Manrope, uppercase, tracked), in khyzr Green behind a short green dash. It opens a section in a khyzr document. khyzr.com's sections carry no eyebrow.

<Eyebrow num="01">What we do</Eyebrow>

One eyebrow to a section, over its title. On dark it turns green-bright. Never a sentence, and never without a title under it.

Props
PropTypeNotes
numstringThe section's number, with its leading zero: "01".
onDarkbooleanOn a dark ground: the label and its dash turn green-bright.
children requiredReact.ReactNode
  • The eyebrow. A numbered label in the technical register, in khyzr-green behind a short green dash. It opens a section in a document built from doc.css. On dark it turns on-dark-accent. khyzr.com's sections carry no eyebrow.
  • The title. text-title at fw-display, on leading-title, tracked at tracking-display. It ends in a period when it is a sentence. One green phrase to a title, at most: the payoff, usually its last words. On dark the title takes on-dark-heading.
  • The lede. text-lede in muted, on leading-lede, held to a short measure. On dark it takes on-dark-body.
  • Split header. The title holds the left and the lede stands at the right, on the title's last line. On a narrow screen the lede drops under the title.

Accents

A small vocabulary, used sparingly.

  • The green dot. A green-300 dot with a soft glow, on dark: a period that matters, or the mark before a closing line. On light it is khyzr-green, small and plain: the marker of where you are.
  • The accent phrase. The payoff words of a line in green: khyzr-green on light, on-dark-accent on dark.
  • The short green rule. A rule 14px to 24px long, in khyzr-green, beside the current item of a list, or before an eyebrow.

Status chips

The chip says what state a thing is in. It is not the logo badge.

The status chip: a small pill in the technical register. It says what state a thing is in. It is not the logo badge.

<Badge tone="solid">Live</Badge> <Badge>In review</Badge> <Badge tone="neutral">Draft</Badge> <Badge tone="mint">KH-04</Badge>

Tones: solid (a settled state) · outline (a state in progress, the default) · neutral (quiet meta) · mint (an identifier or a count). The mint tone sets deep-forest on green-100. One word or two, never a sentence.

Props
PropTypeNotes
tone'solid' | 'outline' | 'neutral' | 'mint'solid = a settled state; outline = a state in progress (default); neutral = quiet meta; mint = an identifier or a count.
children requiredReact.ReactNode
  • Solid is a settled state, outline a state in progress, neutral is quiet meta, and mint an identifier or a count.
  • Mint sets deep-forest on green-100. green-600 on mint does not hold at this size.
  • A chip is text-label at tracking-label, in the technical register. One word or two.

Proof

The proof figure: a number that carries a claim.

The proof figure, as khyzr.com's Trusted by band sets it: an ink numeral at display size, its unit mark in khyzr Green at the same size, and a caption in the technical register a line below, in muted.

<ProofStat value="00" suffix="+" label="Placeholder figure" />

A figure is a real number with its source known. Set two or three side by side, never a wall of them.

Props
PropTypeNotes
value requiredstringThe numeral, as its final value: "24". The page counts it up on first reveal; the primitive sets the value it lands on.
suffixstringThe unit mark, in green at the numeral's own size: "+" or "%".
labelstringThe caption, in the technical register, a line below the numeral.
onDarkbooleanOn a dark ground: a white numeral, a green-bright unit mark.
  • An ink numeral at display size, its unit mark in khyzr-green at the numeral's own size, and a caption a line below, in the technical register, in muted.
  • On dark the numeral takes on-dark-heading, the unit mark green-300, and the caption on-dark-muted.
  • On first reveal a figure counts up from zero over dur-count, then settles with a pulse of 1.07 over dur-pulse. Under reduced motion it shows its final value.
  • A figure is a real number with its source known. Two or three stand side by side, and never a wall of them.
