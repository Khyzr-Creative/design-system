Design system · As built

khyzr.com as built

The record of khyzr.com as it stands: its layout and scale, the values it sets beside the tokens, its marks and pictures, its navigation and sections, the pages beside the homepage, and its floors. The tokens stand. These values are the site's own.

Layout

One column, centered, inside gutters. The column and the type follow the window, so the margins grow with the screen.

The desktop scale

Between 1200px and 2560px wide, khyzr.com scales its type and widens its margins on two curves. The tokens' values are unchanged. The site scales them as one.

WindowRoot sizeMargin, each side
1200px and under16px5%
1440px15.2px7.5%
1680pxabout 14.4px11.4%
1920px13.6px, which is 85%15.5%
2560px and over16px19.5%
  • Sizes are in rem, and the root's size follows the window. From 1920px up, the page is the 2560px page, whole, at a smaller size. Below 1920px each width keeps its layout, a little smaller.
  • A size inside a section that grows with the page grows with the column, and not with the window.
  • A dense screen under 1728px wide stops at 95%. On tablets and phones nothing is scaled.
  • The root's size is stated in px, so a browser's own text size does not move the page. Zoom enlarges it.
  • At 1920px everything is drawn at 85%: body text renders at 12.75px to 14.5px, and the nav's type at 11.5px to 11.9px. Only text-eyebrow and text-label are floored, at 11px.

Section padding

One measure, section-pad-y, and the cases it meets.

  • One measure stands between a section's content and any edge where the ground changes, the same on both sides of that edge.
  • Two neighbors on one ground with nothing between them share the seam, at .6 of a measure each: Disciplines and Alongside, and the manifesto under the hero.
  • A section that ends in its own controls on the next section's ground adds nothing under them. The next section's measure is the seam.
  • The Trusted by band is a strip, and not a section: 40px to 64px above and below.
  • 44px to 72px of fluid space sits under a section's header.
  • The footer follows the close on the same ink: space-4 above its map, 40px below.

Breakpoints

  • There is no global set. Each section restacks where its content needs to: most at 860px or 760px, with a phone step at 640px, which is break-phone.
  • The nav's links leave below 1000px. There is no phone menu: the wordmark and the action remain.
  • A phone turned on its side has a rule of its own, under 500px of height.

Spacing

  • khyzr.com also sets five steps between the scale's: 6px, 10px, 14px, 20px, and 28px. They serve distances inside a component: the gap between two controls, a pill's padding, the space under a card's name. They are the site's own.

  • On dark, type is protected by shade, and not by a panel. Each name in the night field carries a text shadow and a blurred pool of pitch behind it. On khyzr.ai's stage the type sits on a blurred pool of the tile's own color.

The gradient and the orb

  • khyzr.com's hero does not wear the ramp. It is white, with the orb.
  • The orb is given four of the system's greens as its paint: green-100 for its body, leaf for its low tone, green-300 for its mid tone, and green-050 for its high tone. Its light is then mapped onto a duotone from khyzr-green to green-050.
  • The ramp appears once, run the other way, as the dusk that leads into the name section: white at the top, down through green-100, mint, leaf, forest, and green-900 into pitch. The same stops, mirrored, mixed in OKLab.
  • The night field paints its grass in forest, khyzr-green, green-400, mint, and green-300, with lights of its own.

Type

The sizes the built site sets beside the scale. None is a token. They are stated at full size, and each follows the desktop scale.

Faces

  • Outfit at 400, 500, and 600 sets everything: 600 for display, 500 for names inside cards and lists and for the interface, 400 for reading. There is no 700 and no italic.

  • Noto Naskh Arabic at 400, 500, and 600, cut down to the letters used.
  • Instrument Serif is not loaded. The site sets no serif turn.

Reading

  • Lede. text-lede on leading-lede, on a measure counted in characters. The legal pages' lede sits a little looser, at 1.55.
  • Hero tagline. 17px to 20px in muted, on a line-height of 1.5.
  • Body. 15px to 17px around text-body: 15px for the footer's links and the small lines of a list, 15.5px on the discipline cards, 16px for a product's line and the text link, 17px for an insight's summary and the name's gloss. Running text keeps a line-height of 1.55 to 1.6, and legal text 1.65.
  • Small text. 13.5px to 14px in Outfit: the nav's links, the footer's base row, an insight's topic, a partner's lane. These are not the technical register.
  • The name's verses. 18px to 23px, on a line-height of 1.5. Emphasis inside a verse is fw-ui in on-dark-heading.

The name moment

  • The name's one moment is the name section, which opens on its title.
  • The section sets الخضر at display size, at weight 400 in on-dark-accent, under the section's title and over its gloss.
  • The same section's trail carries the name in fifteen languages, each with its language named and three words. الخضر stands there twice more, as the Arabic and the Urdu entries.
  • A name in the trail is drawn to its depth, around 34px at fw-ui, with its language around 15px and its three words around 17px. A phone starts from 24px, 13px, and 15px.
  • Arabic and Farsi set in font-arabic, and Latin scripts in Outfit. The other scripts set in their own Noto faces: Nastaliq Urdu, Sans Devanagari, Sans Tamil, Sans SC, Sans JP, and Sans KR. Each script takes an optical factor against Outfit, from .88 for the CJK faces to 1.14 for Arabic.
  • Those faces are fetched only when the trail shows, and they serve this section alone.
  • No script but Latin is tracked, and الخضر is never split.

Marks

The wordmark signs the page. The symbol builds things on it.

The wordmark

  • The nav carries the wordmark alone: the on-light file over the white hero and on paper, crossfading to the on-dark file over the dark sections.
  • The nav's wordmark follows the desktop scale: 76px wide at 1200px and under and at 2560px and over, 72px at 1440px, and 65px at 1920px. It stands under the 90px floor at every width. It is the one exception, and the floor stands everywhere else.
  • The footer carries the on-dark wordmark, 104px to 124px wide, without the ®.
  • The legal pages' bar carries the on-light wordmark at the nav's size.

The favicon and the share card

  • Every page links the bare on-dark symbol as its favicon. The site carries no PNG fallback, no touch icon, and no manifest, and its theme color is white. The system's own favicon set is on the Assets page.
  • The share card, at 1200 × 630, is the site's own first screen captured at that size: the wordmark at the top left, the orb held at rest, the title, and the tagline, with the nav's links and the hero's actions hidden. The tagline on it sets at 16.8px.

The symbol as geometry

  • The ring. In Products, four cuts of the symbol stand edge to edge, so their quarter rings close into one ring. khyzr's cut wears the on-dark symbol's three greens on a green-900 tile. Each product's cut wears its own three colors on its own deep tile.
  • The fourth cut. The symbol in a single outline, 1.25px wide, in khyzr-green, on paper.
  • khyzr's stage. The symbol tiled without end as a lattice of rings on green-900, in three greens, drifting, with one light traveling from ring to ring. It is a relative of the logo pattern, and the site's own.
  • The blank-page stage. The symbol's bands drawn in a green hairline over the compass work that constructs them. Beside it, the wordmark's letterforms in ink, followed by a period and a blinking green caret.
  • The lost page. The symbol alone, at 84px, in its on-light file's colors.

Other companies' marks

  • The partners' marks set in ink in the ecosystem's list.
  • The clients' marks roll in gray on the light band, and set in white on the voices' dark faces.

Icons

  • Illustrative. Six, one to a discipline card, in forest on a white tile 64px square with a hairline, turning green-300 on a forest tile under the pointer. They set at 36px at full size: 34px at 1440px wide, 32px on a phone, and 30.6px at 1920px, a little under the set's 32px floor.
  • Utility. arrow-right on text links and the next control, its mirror on the previous control, and arrow-right-up on the two links in the footer that leave the site.
  • Outside the set. Four drawings are the site's own: pause and play, filled; the cross that closes a stage; and the lost page's arrow, at a stroke of 1.6.
  • Seven of the system's nine utility icons are not used.

Imagery

  • The page carries two photographs: one beside the Alongside line, at radius-l, and one on the first insight sheet, at radius-m. Both show people mid-work, in daylight, not facing the camera.
  • The grade is baked into the image files, so no filter runs on the page. A photograph fades in over 700ms once it has loaded.
  • Grounds are flat color everywhere but the dusk, the night field, and the stages.
  • khyzr.com carries neither of the system's two patterns. Its one relative of them is khyzr's stage.

  • The bar turns to its ink state from 40% of the way down the dusk, and stays so over the name, the close, and the footer. The wordmark crossfades to its on-dark file with it.
  • The links are pills at fw-ui, centered in the bar.
  • The link of the section on screen is marked. It takes ink, or white on dark, and a 4px dot under it: khyzr-green on light, green-300 on dark.

The scrollbar

  • A rail at the window's right edge, 2px wide, in ink at 10%, with a khyzr-green thumb on it and the sections ticked along it in ink at 22%. Under the pointer the rail and the thumb widen, to 4px and 6px. Drag the thumb, or click the rail.
  • The rail starts under the nav. The thumb is never shorter than 44px.
  • While the page scrolls, or under the pointer, the section on screen names itself beside the thumb, in muted.
  • Over the dark sections the scrollbar turns with the nav: the thumb takes green-300, the rail and the ticks white at 14% and 30%, and the label on-dark-muted on ink at 55%.
  • It serves a mouse and a trackpad only. A touch screen, and a page without JavaScript, keep the browser's own bar. A machine with two cores or 2GB of memory, or less, gets the label without its blur.

Sections

The sections as built, in page order.

Hero

  • A full screen of white, never under 720px tall on a desktop, with everything centered a little above the middle: the orb, the title, the tagline, and two pills, the primary and the secondary.
  • The orb's slot is at most 430px across, and smaller on a short or a narrow screen. The orb rests while the hero is out of view.

Manifesto

  • One statement on white, held to measure-reading, set as letterpress: pressed blind, then inked.
  • It shares its seam with the hero.

Trusted by

  • A light band with a hairline below. A pair of proof figures, each the ProofStat primitive, stands off from the roll behind a hairline.
  • Client marks roll beside them in a 30-second loop, fading out at both edges. Each takes a height of its own, and they stand 56px to 110px apart.
  • The roll pauses under the pointer and sleeps off screen. A round pause and play control stands at its end.
  • On a phone the pair sits over the roll, and the marks draw at 80%.

Discipline cards

  • Six cards on paper, three by two.
  • A card rests on surface-2 at radius-l, without a border. It carries its discipline's icon on a white tile, then the name at fw-ui and one line in muted.
  • Under the pointer the card comes up to the paper: white, a hairline, a 4px lift, and shadow-lift.
  • The disciplines are not numbered.

Alongside

  • A photograph on seven columns of twelve, and beside it a line set as a title, its last words in green, with one paragraph in muted.
  • It shares its seam with Disciplines.

Principles

  • On surface-2. The title and the lede stand at the left. At the right, a display quote whose second sentence is green, and whose words come up as it scrolls.
  • The principles are a row of five cards: paper, a hairline, radius-m, each with its numeral, 01 to 05, and its name set vertically.
  • Hover, focus, or a tap opens one. It widens and turns green-900, showing its name and one line in the on-dark colors, with the numeral in on-dark-accent. One principle rests open.

Voices

  • A facade of vertical prisms, edge to edge like a wall, with three faces for three voices. It is fluted, so it reads as slats at rest. A turn rotates each prism to its next face, slat after slat.
  • The faces are ink, green-900, and mint: on-dark type on the first two, and ink on mint. The quote sets in Outfit at display weight. Under it stand the client's mark, the name at fw-ui, and the role.
  • The controls sit under the facade, on paper: a round pause and play control, and a button for each voice with a swatch of its face. A khyzr-green bar under the current voice shows its time.
  • The section is switched off on khyzr.com. The pattern stands.

Ecosystem

  • The partners are typeset as one list, and not as cards: each a mark, its name at fw-ui, its lane, and one line.
  • A single brace gathers the list into one statement at display weight. On a phone the brace turns under the list.

Products

  • On surface-2. The ring is 300px to 640px across.
  • The title and each product's line sit at the corner beside its cut, each with a text link.
  • A cut is a control. Under the pointer it lifts a hair, a soft shadow falls under it, and a light runs along its quarter of the ring. It opens a stage in its own colors, closed by a round control 48px across.
  • Inside a stage, the focus ring takes that stage's own accent.

Insights

  • Three notes as sheets at radius-m: paper with a hairline; green-050 with a green-100 border; and green-900 in the on-dark colors, with the white pill.
  • A sheet carries its topic in khyzr-green, a display title, a summary in muted, and a pill 44px tall. The first carries a photograph.
  • The sheets stack as a pile. Throw the top one aside by dragging it, with the previous and next controls, or with the arrow keys. A list of topics beside the pile names the note on top, marked by a short green rule, and brings any note up.
  • A sheet under the pile shows its paper, and not its words.

Floors

What khyzr.com keeps when the screen is a phone, the pointer is a finger, or a script does not run.

Phone and touch

  • On a touch screen every target is a finger's size, 44px: the nav's pill, a footer link, a topic, a voice. A text link's hit area is 44px tall at every width.
  • A hover that moves or hides something applies only where the pointer can hover. A press answers every pointer, and no gray flash covers a pill under a finger.
  • On a phone the hero is the screen it is seen on, and grows only if its content needs more. On a phone turned on its side the orb stands beside the words.

Keyboard

  • Every control takes the focus ring: focus-width in focus-ring, focus-offset away, and white on dark.
  • The skip link is the first stop. The pile answers the arrow keys, and a principle opens on focus.
