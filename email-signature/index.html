Each form follows as you type, as it will arrive. A phone number is yours to leave out.

  • For new emails: the whole signature. Set it as your default.
  • For replies and forwards: the short form, your name and your role at the brand.
  • As plain text: for a phone's mail app, which keeps its own signature, and for mail sent as plain text.

Paste it in.

Make one signature for new emails and one for replies, and set each as the default for its own kind of message.

Gmail

  1. Open Settings, then See all settings.
  2. Under General, find Signature. Choose Create new and name it after your brand: khyzr, khyzr.ai, or khyzr.studio.
  3. Paste. Do the same for the short form, and name it the same with reply after it: khyzr reply.
  4. Under Signature defaults, set the signature for new emails and the short form for replies and forwards.
  5. Go to the bottom of the page and choose Save changes.

Outlook

  1. On Windows: File, Options, Mail, Signatures. In the new Outlook and on the web: Settings, Accounts, Signatures.
  2. Choose New, name it after your brand, and paste.
  3. Do the same for the short form, and name it the same with reply after it.
  4. Set the signature for new messages and the short form for replies and forwards. Save.

How it is built.

A signature is text first. It reads in any mail program, with pictures on or off.

  • The name, the role, one phone number or none, the brand's site, and its wordmark. No email address: the reader has it.
  • Set in Arial, since an email cannot carry Outfit. See Type.
  • No tagline, no banner, no second logo.
  • khyzr signs in ink, with khyzr.com in khyzr Green and the green wordmark.
  • khyzr.ai and khyzr.studio sign in the near-black of their own wordmark, and carry no green. See The house.
  • A product's wordmark stands on a small white plate, so it still reads when a mail program turns dark. On white the plate does not show.
  • The wordmark is one picture, loaded from this site, so the email carries no attachment. Where pictures are off, its place reads the brand's name.
