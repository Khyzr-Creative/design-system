الخضر

Arabic font-arabic

Noto Naskh Arabic · الخضر, once per surface

  • Outfit, display (font-display). The hero word, titles, ledes, quotes, pillars, taglines. Geometric, tight tracking. Locked as the display face on 2026-07-08.
  • Outfit, text and UI (font-text). Body copy, buttons, nav links, captions. The workhorse — everything that isn't display or a technical label.
  • Manrope, the technical register (font-tech). Eyebrows, labels, numerals and indices, lanes, footer headings, the scroll cue, chart axes and captions. Small technical text only, 11–13px, never a sentence. Always uppercase, always wide-tracked — the only place uppercase is allowed outside the hero word. Section eyebrows are numbered in this register: "01 · What we do".
  • Instrument Serif, the editorial accent (font-serif-accent). Italic only, lowercase — one human moment per page. Never for body copy or headings.
  • Noto Naskh Arabic, the name (font-arabic). Reserved for the name moment — الخضر, Al-Khidr, "the Green One." It appears at most once per surface, and nowhere else. Browser and app chrome are exempt.

No monospace

  • JetBrains Mono is retired; the system has no monospace. font-mono survives only as a deprecated alias of font-tech — use font-tech.
  • Space Grotesk is not part of the system.
  • The product sites follow the same decision: khyzr.ai and khyzr.studio are Outfit-only and load no Manrope. khyzr.com keeps its eyebrows in Manrope.
  • Webfonts are Google-hosted for now; there are no local font files yet.

Rules

How type behaves.

One green word

Titles get at most one word in khyzr Green — usually the last, usually the payoff: in "One team.", the green falls on "team."

Sentence case everywhere

Headings and body are sentence case, and headlines end with periods when they are sentences. Uppercase belongs to the technical labels and the hero word only.

Tight up top, open below

Display tracks negative, −0.02 to −0.03em; titles take tracking-display. Technical labels track wide — tracking-eyebrow on eyebrows, .10em on labels. Body stays neutral. Manrope sets wider than the retired mono did, so its tracking came down with the change.

Serif is a spice

Instrument Serif italic appears once per page at most — a human aside, never structure.

Weights by role

Weights are set by role, never by eye: fw-display for display, fw-ui for UI, fw-text for prose.
