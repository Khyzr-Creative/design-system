Documents for builders

The two document classes as they are built: one stylesheet, one element, the anatomy of each page, and the blocks a page is composed from. What a khyzr document is, and when to use which class, is the brand's Documents chapter.

The stylesheet and the element

  • One stylesheet, doc.css, styles both classes, and one element, <doc-page>, lays them out.
  • Every color, face, weight, and radius in them comes from the tokens. A token that changes reaches every document the next time it is built.
  • The two classes are the document, one flowing column, and the brand book, designed page by page.
  • The templates are not served on this site. They travel with the brand kit.
  • The eyebrow belongs to the documents built from doc.css. The Word and Google Docs templates, the decks, and the khyzr brand book set no label over a heading.

Shared anatomy

Both classes are built from the same parts.

The document, page by page

One column of Outfit at 14.5px, on a line-height of 1.65, under a header and a footer that repeat on every page.

PartAs built
Running headerThe on-light wordmark at the left, 92px wide. At the right, the document's label, at text-label in faint
Running footerOver a hairline rule: the green dot and the document's title at the left, and where to ask at the right. Both at text-label in faint
OpeningThe eyebrow, the title at 40px on a line-height of 1.05, then one lede at 16px in muted, held to 560px
Section heading22px, tracked −.015em, under a hairline rule. A sub-heading is 15px
TextA link is khyzr-green, the value link holds, with no underline. Emphasis is 600 in ink
Closing lineOne line at 13.5px in muted: where to ask, or where to go next
  • A title and a section heading end with a period: "Color palette.", "The greens." A card heading does not.
  • Hairlines do the separating. A section opens on a rule, and not on a larger heading.
  • A heading never ends a page. Cards, rows, steps, and swatches travel whole, and a long block can open a page of its own.
  • The green dot is 5px, in khyzr-green.
  • On the employee shelf, a document's eyebrow and its running label name its folder.

The brand book, page by page

A page is white, green-900, or ink, padded 60px at the top, 64px at the sides, and 88px at the foot. Text runs at 14.5px, on a line-height of 1.6.

PageAs built
Covergreen-900. At the top, the on-dark wordmark, 126px wide, and the document's label at text-label in on-dark-faint. The hero word in Outfit at fw-display: 108px, uppercase, on a line-height of .94, tracked −.025em, white, ending on a period in green-300. Under it, a subtitle at 16.5px in on-dark-soft, held to 400px, with one serif turn in white. At the right, the cover column: a still of the mesh in its dark look, 230px wide, at radius-l. At the foot, the meta grid
Chapter pageWhite. The ghost numeral at the top right, in Outfit at fw-display: 150px, tracked −.03em. Then the eyebrow, the title at 44px on a line-height of 1.04, a lede held to 640px where one is needed, and the blocks
Statementgreen-900. The document's one sentence, in quotation marks, in Outfit at fw-display: 50px, on a line-height of 1.1, tracked −.022em, white, held to 640px. Supporting text in on-dark-soft, held to 560px
Back coverink. Centered: the white badge, 64px high, one question at 30px in white, and one contact card at radius-m with an on-dark-divider border: the brand's address. The foot carries the tagline at the right
One-page sheetA green-900 title band: the on-dark wordmark, 96px wide, beside the title at 30px in white, its period in green-300, with one line at the right. The sheet runs smaller, at 12.5px, under kickers in khyzr-green. A note line at 11px in muted, then an ink foot with the tagline at the right
  • The meta grid. One entry over an on-dark-divider rule: where to send questions. Its key is in the technical register in on-dark-muted, and its value at 13.5px in on-dark-soft.
  • The page foot. 40px above the bottom edge, over a hairline rule: the green dot and the document's title at the left, and the page count at the right, as 02 / 05. On a dark page the rule is on-dark-divider and the type on-dark-faint. The cover and the back cover carry none.
  • The ghost numeral repeats the number of the eyebrow that opens the page. It sits in green-100 at 55% opacity.
  • A statement appears once in a document, at most.
  • The tagline signs every back cover and every sheet.

Color, pattern, and imagery

  • A highlight. One word marked with marigold at 28% behind it, where a document needs to point at a single term.
  • The cover column. The brand book's cover column holds a still of the mesh in its dark look, the A4 portrait file, cropped to the column (see the pattern elements). A document takes a still, never the live element: paper does not move.
  • Imagery. Honest documentary at radius-m, never sharing a band with a background. Most documents need no photograph: restraint is the default.
  • Logos. Shown on tiles: white with a hairline border, green-900, and pitch where a mark is shown on black.
