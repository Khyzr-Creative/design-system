A set for each product.

khyzr.ai and khyzr.studio each have a presentation and a social set of their own, in the product's colors.

  • They are named the same way: "khyzr.ai presentation", "khyzr.studio presentation".
  • They live in the product's own folder, with its logos and its guide.
  • Letters and cards come from khyzr's own templates.

How a file is named.

A file's name says what it is, because a file leaves its folder when someone downloads it.

  • Plain words, beginning with khyzr, in lowercase: "khyzr presentation", "khyzr brand book", "khyzr quick guide".
  • No version, no date, no "final", no "new", no initials. A file is updated by replacing it, so its name and its link never change.
  • Logo, background, and color files follow a stricter pattern, since they are typed into tools: khyzr-wordmark_on-light.svg, khyzr-mesh_dark_slide_1920x1080.jpg. See Logo files.

The brand kit.

The same files as a download, for partners, agencies, and anyone building with the brand.

  • Three packs: one for khyzr, one for khyzr.ai, one for khyzr.studio.
  • Each holds that brand's logos in every format, its colors, its backgrounds, its guide, and the fonts. khyzr's also holds the brand book.
  • Builders pin a release of the kit, so their work does not move under them.

For AI tools.

The design system travels as one download, so an AI tool works from the real rules and the real files.

  • khyzr-design-system.zip: the system's own folder, with a note at its root on what it is and how to use it.
  • A one-page brief to paste into an AI tool sits beside it.
  • Both are in the Drive, under For designers and developers.
