06 · Expression

Icons

One icon family, Solar, in two weights — Linear for the utility tier, Line Duotone for the illustrative tier. Utility works; illustrative speaks.

Two tiers

One family — Solar, by 480 Design (MIT) — in two weights.

Utility · Solar Linear

Quiet strokes that do work: navigation, actions, form states.

  • 16–24px, stroke 1.5, a single color.
  • ink or body on light; white or green-bright (green-300) on dark.
  • Inline with text or in buttons.

Illustrative · Solar Line Duotone

The same geometry plus a 20% layer, reserved for feature moments: discipline markers, section leads, empty states.

  • 32px or larger, one per card or section.
  • forest on light, green-bright on dark. The 20% layer rides along.

Fifteen icons, on purpose

The set covers what the site actually uses — nothing speculative. Nine utility icons, six illustrative.

Utility

A 24 grid, stroke 1.5, currentColor.

Illustrative

Semantic names, and the swap target for bespoke icons.

  • Illustrative files carry semantic names: strategy.svg, not compass.svg.
  • When bespoke icons arrive, they replace files one for one, and every surface updates.

Color, size, restraint

DoDon't
Inherit color through currentColor — ink or body on light, white or green-bright on dark, forest for duotone on lightMix suites — no Lucide, Phosphor, or leftover glyph fonts
Utility at 16–24px; illustrative at 32px or largerShrink illustrative icons below 32px — the 20% layer muddies
One illustrative icon per card or section — it marks the momentRecolor the duotone layer independently, or add fills
Keep semantic filenames; bespoke icons replace files one for oneUse icons for brand marks — LinkedIn, X and partners are logos
  • No emoji, and no unicode characters standing in for icons.

In use

  • Discipline markers take the illustrative icons, in duotone, green-bright on dark.
  • Row and link arrows are arrow-right.
  • The external-link mark is arrow-right-up.
  • Both arrows replace the typed → and ↗.

Files

  • Masters live in design-system/assets/icons/, split into utility/ and illustrative/.
  • Every file is sized 1em wide and high and draws in currentColor.
