Icons

One icon family, Solar, in two weights. Small line icons do work. Larger two-tone icons mark a moment.

One family, two weights.

Every khyzr icon comes from Solar. The line weight works. The two-tone weight speaks.

  • Line icons are quiet strokes that do a job: an arrow, a close, a check. Small, in one color, beside text or inside a button.
  • Two-tone icons are the same shapes with a soft second tone, for a moment that matters: a discipline, the head of a section, an empty state.
  • Fifteen are ready, and they cover what khyzr uses today. The rest of the family is on the Drive, and in the brand kit.

Size and color.

An icon takes the color of the type beside it.

LineTwo-tone
Size on screen16 to 24px32px or larger
On lightInk, or the gray of the textForest
On darkWhite, or light greenLight green
How manyAs the work needsOne to a card or a section
  • Never a secondary color, and never more than two tones.
  • Every icon on a slide or a page is the same size.

Two good examples.

An icon earns its place by making something faster to find. If it only decorates, leave it out.

  • A line icon at work. The arrow at the end of a text link: "See the work". In ink, at the size of the type beside it.
  • A two-tone icon at work. One icon at the head of a card that names a discipline: Strategy, Finance, Operations. In Forest, on white, one to a card.

What not to do.

The family stays whole, and the icons stay icons.

  • Don't mix in another icon family.
  • Don't shrink a two-tone icon below 32px. Its second tone turns to mud.
  • Don't recolor the second tone on its own, add a fill, or add a shadow.
  • Don't stretch or rotate an icon.
  • Don't use an icon for a company's mark. LinkedIn, X, and partners are logos.
  • Don't build an icon from the khyzr symbol, or set one where the mark should be.
  • No emoji, and no typed characters standing in for icons.

Icons for slides and documents.

The fifteen ship as ready images, already colored: one set for light backgrounds, one for dark.

  • Line icons are 96px square, in ink or white. Two-tone icons are 256px square, in Forest or light green.
  • A file is named in plain words for what it shows: Arrow right.png, Strategy.png.
  • Scale an icon by its corner, so it stays square.
  • They are in the Drive's icons folder, with their vector originals. See Templates and files.

The products keep their own.

This page binds everything that signs as khyzr: its site, its decks, its documents.

  • khyzr.ai and khyzr.studio each keep the icons their own site is built with. One family to a product, and never mixed with Solar.
  • A two-tone icon is named for what it means, not what it shows: strategy.svg, not compass.svg. When khyzr commissions its own icons, they replace these files one for one.

Where the icons come from.

khyzr's icons are one family, Solar.

  • The Drive carries the fifteen as vector files, and the whole Solar library in the two weights khyzr uses.
  • An icon taken from the library stays in its weight for the whole piece: line for the small working ones, two-tone for the large ones.
