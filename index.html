Design system

Everything starts here. The khyzr design system holds the tokens, assets, components and thirteen chapters of rules that every khyzr surface is built from.

Start with colorGet the logos

How to use this

  • Tokens are the values. Color, type, spacing, radii and motion are set in the token files, design-system/tokens/. Where any prose — a chapter, the digest, an older document — disagrees with a token, the token wins.
  • Logos are used as files. Take the exported SVGs from design-system/assets/logos/. Never redraw a mark, recolor it, or compose symbol and name by hand. Mark colors come from the mark files, palette colors from the tokens.
  • The site is built from the system's own files. Every swatch, specimen, download and live component here is generated from the tokens, the logo, icon and pattern files, and the React primitives. Nothing is copied by hand, so nothing can drift.
  • Start from the tokens. design-system/styles.css is the CSS entry and imports every token file; link it and every value on this site is yours. The five React primitives run live on the Components and Forms pages, with their props.
  • It is a working system. Decisions land at locked checkpoints. Where something has no answer yet, it says so and stays pending.

What's inside

Thirteen chapters in four parts, and a fifth part with a page for each product.

Foundations — how it's built

  • 01 Foundations — the layout grid and fluid gutters, radii, hairlines and shadows, spacing rhythm.
  • 02 Color — the Verdant standard: a deep green lead; cobalt, marigold, terracotta and steel secondaries; the functional scale; measured accessibility.
  • 03 Type — Outfit for display, text and UI; Manrope for small technical labels; one serif accent.
  • 04 Voice — four traits, signature moves, CTA mechanics, the lexicon and the name-usage rule.

Expression — how it looks and feels

  • 05 Logos — wordmark, symbol and lockups: usage, clearspace, minimum sizes, the ® set.
  • 06 Icons — Solar in two weights: Linear for utility, Line Duotone for feature moments. Semantic filenames, ready for a bespoke swap.
  • 07 Imagery — honest documentary: real people mid-work, a calm grade, never green-tinted. Interim stock rules and the launch shoot list.
  • 08 Pattern — the official cutout texture: the <khyzr-pattern> asset, its themes, rules and section-by-section usage.
  • 09 Motion — the Green Trace. Things grow in: root-anchored rises with a breath of overshoot, overlapping staggers, settle pulses. Springs move things, never colors.

Interface — what users touch

  • 10 Components — every site pattern recreated one to one: buttons, navigation, cards, bands.
  • 11 Forms — Plate fields, quiet at rest and white with a green ring on focus. States, controls and the on-ink companion.
  • 12 Data — cobalt and signal. The Canopy grammar: green carries the data, cobalt and steel step back, and one terracotta flag says look here — never a series, never UI chrome.

Documents

  • 13 Documents — how khyzr paper reads: two document classes, one anatomy — a white canvas, the wordmark header, brand-book covers. Duplicate a template; never restyle from scratch.

Products

  • 14 khyzr.ai — AI agents in the client's own AWS account: the product mark, its periwinkle-to-aqua ramp on a near-black ground, and what it shares with khyzr.studio.
  • 15 khyzr.studio — design on subscription: the product mark, the ember ramp on a warm ground with the .studio yellow as its colour, and what it shares with khyzr.ai.
