khyzr

Brand

For everyone. Who khyzr is, and how it looks and sounds anywhere: the brand and its voice, the logo, color, type, backgrounds, imagery, icons, and data, the house and its two products, and the brand in use.

Opening

  1. 01Start hereThe khyzr brand guidelines. What they are, who they are for, the few rules that matter most, and where the files are.

Who we are

  1. 02The brandWho khyzr is. The name and what it carries, why we exist, the three pillars, the personality, and the tagline.
  2. 03VoiceHow khyzr sounds and how its copy is written. The personality, the eight rules, how a sentence lands, the mechanics, the words, and the name.

The look

  1. 04LogosThe marks of khyzr and how to place them. The name leads. The symbol stands alone only where the name cannot fit.
  2. 05ColorInk and white lead. One green carries the brand, and it is rare. Four secondaries each have one job.
  3. 06TypeOne family does the work. Outfit sets every title, sentence, and button. Three other faces each have one small job.
  4. 07BackgroundsA dial from quiet to expressive. White is always right. The mesh is the default background. The logo pattern is very expressive, so it is used sparingly.
  5. 08ImageryHonest documentary. Real people mid-work, real rooms, natural light. Photography is the evidence that we are actually there.
  6. 09IconsOne icon family, Solar, in two weights. Small line icons do work. Larger two-tone icons mark a moment.
  7. 10DataGreen carries, terracotta flags. A khyzr chart says one thing at a time, and reads as khyzr before its legend does.

The house

  1. 11The housekhyzr is a branded house. khyzr.ai and khyzr.studio keep its name, its marks, and its typeface, and each brings a color of its own.
  2. 12khyzr.aikhyzr.ai keeps khyzr's name, wordmark, symbol, and typeface. What is its own is a black ground and one ramp of light, periwinkle to aqua, that appears only on what is active.
  3. 13khyzr.studiokhyzr.studio keeps khyzr's name, wordmark, symbol, and typeface. What is its own is a black ground, bone and ash for the words, and ember, five warm steps from yellow to rose, that arrives as an event.

In use

  1. 14In useThe brand at work. khyzr's own pieces, each shown as a finished object, with where to find its template.
  2. 15DocumentsHow khyzr paper reads. Two kinds of document, one anatomy. Start from a template, and never restyle from scratch.

Appendix

  1. 16Color valuesEvery khyzr color with its values, for designers and printers. Once for screens, once for print, with what shifts in ink said plainly.
  2. 17Logo filesEvery logo file behind the marks. Which file for which job, how a file is named, what each pack holds, and the smallest size of every mark.
  3. 18Type in each toolThe typefaces as files. What each font is called in a menu, how to install it, how it travels with a file, and what steps in when it is missing.
  4. 19Motion as a principleHow khyzr moves, as an idea. Things grow in. The curves and the clocks are in the design system.
  5. 20Templates and filesWhat exists and where. The templates, how a file is named, the Drive that holds everything, and the brand kit.
  6. 21LegalThe registered mark and the lines that go with it. A record of what is written down, not legal advice.
  7. 22Who to askWhere a question about the brand goes, and what is approved before it is used.
