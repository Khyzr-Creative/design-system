Brand · The look

Logos

The marks of khyzr and how to place them. The name leads. The symbol stands alone only where the name cannot fit.

The wordmark leads.

The wordmark is khyzr's signature. It is the default mark on every page, slide, and screen.

  • It comes in two cuts: one for white and light grounds, one for dark grounds.
  • A mark is placed from its file. It is never redrawn, retyped, or recolored.
  • The glowing green dot is an accent motif, not a brand mark.

The symbol, where the name cannot fit.

The symbol stands alone only in tight squares: a favicon, an avatar, an app tile, a closing mark.

  • It holds from 24px up. Below that, the badge carries it.
  • The symbol is never shown in one color. Its greens are the mark.

Lockups, where there is room.

Symbol and name together, for generous room: a cover, a title slide, a letterhead, a sign.

  • The horizontal lockup sets them side by side. The vertical lockup is for square and tall formats.
  • Never compose symbol and name by hand. New lockups are cut from the masters only, on request.

The badge, for the smallest sizes.

Under 24px the symbol appears only on a plate: a favicon, an avatar, an app tile.

  • The default plate is the deep green.
  • On black or near-black surroundings the plate is white.
  • For an avatar the badge fills the square, edge to edge, and the platform rounds it. Never the bare symbol.
  • The badge is the full-color mark on its plate. It is not a one-color mark.

One color means the wordmark.

Where color cannot print or render, the wordmark goes to black or white. Engraving, embossing, foil, single-color print.

  • The black wordmark goes on light grounds, and the white one on dark grounds.
  • There is no one-color symbol, and none will be cut.
  • The black is a soft black and the white an off-white. Both come with the file.

Give it room.

Clear space is one blade of the symbol, on every side. A blade is one of the symbol's four small arcs. Nothing enters that room: no type, no edge, no other mark.

  • Around the wordmark alone there is no blade to measure, so the room is the height of its z.
  • In a lockup, measure the blade of its own symbol.
  • A file is cut to its ink. No clear space is built in, so leave it yourself.

Never smaller than this.

Each mark has a floor, once for screens and once for print.

MarkOn screenIn print
Wordmark90px wide20 mm wide
Symbol24px8 mm
Badge16px6 mm
Horizontal lockup120px wide30 mm wide
Vertical lockup120px wide20 mm wide
  • Below a mark's floor, use the next mark down: the wordmark, then the symbol, then the badge.
  • Engraving, embroidery, foil, and screen print take the one-color wordmark at 30 mm wide or more.
  • The floors for the ® set and the product marks are on the brand site, under Logo files.

Where the mark sits.

The mark holds the left edge. One khyzr mark to a surface is enough.

  • A page. Top left, in the header. A cover with a picture carries it bottom left, on the white under the picture.
  • A slide. Bottom left on every working slide, and on the cover, on the white under the picture. On the closing slide, top left.
  • A screen. Top left, the wordmark alone.
  • A lockup may sit centered on a cover or a card. The wordmark alone stays left.
  • The tagline is set as type, apart from the mark. There is no tagline lockup.

The right file for each ground.

The ground decides the file.

GroundUse

  • Where a layout allows, keep the mark off the background and on the white beside it.
  • The share card is the one ready piece that sets the color lockup on the mesh. It is placed on the field's darkest part. Use it as it is.

What not to do.

Eight ways to break the mark, each as wrong as the last.

  • Don't stretch or squeeze it.
  • Don't recolor it or tint it.
  • Don't set the symbol in one color.
  • Don't build your own lockup.
  • Don't add a shadow, an outline, or a glow.
  • Don't tilt it or turn it.
  • Don't set it on the gradient's greens, or straight on a photograph.
  • Don't crowd it.

Color in the marks.

The marks carry their own four greens: a brighter cut of the brand's hue than the palette holds. A mark shows three of the four, and which three depends on its ground.

  • Take mark colors from the mark files and palette colors from the palette. Never recolor either to match the other.
  • No tints, no strokes, no recolored versions beyond this set.
  • One exception: the logo pattern. It repeats the symbol's shapes, turned row by row and drawn in one ink, tone on tone, as a background (see Backgrounds). It is a pattern, not a mark: a surface that carries it still carries a real logo file where a logo is needed. Every other rule on this page stands.
  • The values are with the color values.

The registered mark.

The mark is registered. Day to day, use the files without the ®.

  • A second set carries the ®, for where the use is prominent, formal, or legal: contracts, packaging, a first prominent use.
  • Never in a navigation bar.
  • A ® file needs more size than its plain twin, so the ® still reads. Its floors are on the brand site, under Logo files.
  • The ® set is shown on the Legal page.

Product marks.

khyzr.ai and khyzr.studio each carry a product wordmark and a product icon. Each product's own page holds its colors and cuts: khyzr.ai and khyzr.studio.

  • Icon or wordmark, never both. There are no product lockups. The icon covers favicons, avatars, app tiles, and square spaces. The wordmark covers everything else.
  • The wordmark is khyzr's wordmark, unchanged, followed by .ai or .studio.
  • The icon is the khyzr symbol in the product's own colors. Like the symbol, it is never one color.
  • The tile follows the badge: a deep tile in the product's own hue. The white tile is for an avatar on a platform that is itself black. On a product's own black pages the icon keeps its deep tile.

The icons in use

The icon is the tab, the app tile, and the avatar. Wherever the name has room, the wordmark takes over.

  • A product wordmark is never smaller than 20px tall on screen, or 5 mm in print.
  • A product icon holds from 24px and 8 mm. Under that it sits on its tile, down to 16px and 6 mm.
  • A product's browser tab keeps the deep tile in light and dark browsers alike.

Other companies' marks.

khyzr keeps client and partner marks to credit the people it works with, and for nothing else.

  • Credit, never co-branding. khyzr's mark leads. Another company's mark appears as a fact about the work. No partner logos in khyzr lockups, no shared marks, no dual-brand headers.
  • A mark on a slide is a claim. It appears only where the relationship is in writing.
  • Set them on white, as plates. Each is trimmed and aligned, so a row sits evenly. Size them to look equal, not to measure equal, and give each at least its own height of room on every side.
  • Never on a photograph, never on a background pattern. khyzr's own mark sits apart from the row.
  • Never recolor another company's mark: not to khyzr Green, not to gray, and to white only where that company publishes a white version.
  • A new mark comes from the company's own brand or press page, as SVG or EPS, and is approved before it appears anywhere public. See who to ask.
  • How a credit is worded is on the Voice page.
